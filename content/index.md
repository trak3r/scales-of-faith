> *"I will entertain a plethora of outlandish theories, but supposing the existence of a creature from children's fairy tales is a bit too far."*
> — [[The Sage]]

**Scales of Faith** is a system-neutral fantasy campaign setting with one twist: **there are no dragons.** They are folklore, bedtime stories, the fantasy equivalent of Bigfoot or the Loch Ness Monster. An adult who believes in dragons is laughed out of the tavern.

But scattered across the world are strange statues: a lichen-covered boulder in a forgotten cavern, a reef shaped like something in flight, a jungle idol of a winged snake. Two secret orders know what they really are, and they are racing to reach them first: one to destroy them, the other to bring them back.

The party starts out as hired hands in a remote river town and gets caught between them.

## System

Scales of Faith is written for any classic fantasy rules set, such as Shadowdark, OSR games or 5e. Creatures come with descriptions and tactics plus a suggestion of which stock monster to use. There are no stat blocks.
