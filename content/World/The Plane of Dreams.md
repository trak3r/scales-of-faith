*Where rescued dragons wait for a safer world.*

> [!secret]- GM Only
> - The [[Calla Cabal]] sends every dragon it revives to the Plane of Dreams, to wait until the world is safe for its return.
> - Before reviving a dragon, the Cabal opens a portal to the Plane of Dreams, so a previously rescued dragon can greet the newcomer and help with the transition.
> - The [[Dragon Eye Orb]] shows a glimpse of the dragons there: a bright neon sky full of blurry flying silhouettes, deep rumbling voices too muddled to understand, and the smell of sweet lavender mixed with harsh burning sulfur.
> - This is where the dragons' debate, and eventually their civil war, begins ([[Fate of the Dragons]]).
