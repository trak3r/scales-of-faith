*The nameless wizard who ended the War of the Wyrm.*

> [!secret]- GM Only
> - He cast the spell of [[The Stoning]] and was left in a vegetative state.
> - He was **hidden away** from the public eye.
> - There is **no formal record of his name or appearance**. He is simply The Savior.
> - Nobody knows how long he survived, or where his **final resting place** is.
> - The [[Order of the Orchid]] reveres him through its holy book. Rumor says the book encodes the secret of his power source, though that's unlikely, since it was written after his sacrifice. See [[The Founding of the Order]].
> - [[The Survivor]] may once have been his friend or respected associate, and may have been warned to be "off world" when the spell was cast.
