*The war between dragons and men that nobody remembers.*

> [!secret]- GM Only
> ![[the-war-of-the-wyrm.jpg]]
>
> - Long ago, there were dragons.
> - A great leader of nations declared war between dragons and men.
> - The war raged for **centuries**.
> - It ended only with [[The Stoning]].
>
> Today the war survives only in the Order of the Orchid's holy book ([[The Founding of the Order]]) and in what the revived dragons themselves remember ([[Fate of the Dragons]]).
