*The Order's secret crusade against the statues.*

> [!secret]- GM Only
> ![[the-purge.jpg]]
>
> - With no more living dragons to battle, the [[Order of the Orchid]] made it its mission to **find and destroy every stone dragon**.
> - Its knights carry historical records of nearby dragon lairs and smash the statues with a *Holy Word of Power* (see [[Spells]]).
> - Centuries later nobody believes in dragons anymore, so the Order **keeps its mission secret**, lest it be ridiculed for chasing fairy tales or lend credence to the [[Calla Cabal]].
> - The Purge is far along. Only a few stone dragons remain, which is why every statue the party hears of becomes a race.
