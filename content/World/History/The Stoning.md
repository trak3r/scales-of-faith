*One spell that turned every living dragon to stone.*

> [!secret]- GM Only
> - A brilliant wizard, now known only as [[The Savior]], discovered a way to harness an enormous amount of arcane power.
> - He used it to cast a **single spell** that turned **every living dragon to stone instantaneously**, wherever it was: sleeping in a cavern, swimming underwater, in mid-flight.
> - Casting the spell left him in a **vegetative state**.
> - One dragon escaped: [[The Survivor]] happened to be on another plane of existence at that moment.
>
> **Why it matters at the table:**
> - Every [[The Stone Dragons|stone dragon]] is frozen exactly as it was in that instant. That's why the statues are so lifelike and so oddly posed.
> - A revived dragon experiences **no passage of time**. One moment its race was thriving, and the next, strange humans claim it is nearly extinct and a thousand years have passed. See [[Fate of the Dragons]].
