*How a holy book became a church.*

> [!secret]- GM Only
> - After [[The Stoning]], a book in the style of a "new testament" was written about the [[The War of the Wyrm|War of the Wyrm]] and [[The Savior]].
> - A group of zealots adopted it as their **Holy Book** and formed the [[Order of the Orchid]].
> - Rumor says the secret of The Savior's power source is encoded in the book's text, and many historians have tried to crack it. It's unlikely, since the book was written after The Savior's sacrifice.
> - Every [[Knight of the Order]] carries a copy and reads from it nightly.
