*What everyone knows: there are no dragons.*

## The Common View

- Dragons are **folklore**, a fairy tale for children.
- Believing in dragons as an adult is considered childish, and believers are openly **ridiculed**.
- Dragons sit alongside other cryptids and tall tales. Some people claim to believe in them, much as some people in our world believe in Bigfoot or the Loch Ness Monster, but there is **no evidence**.

## Believers and Skeptics

The campaign is full of people at different points on that spectrum:

- [[Gordon the Dragonologist]] insists dragons are real, just invisible and silent. He charges for consultations.
- [[Brock Blusterhaus]] tells drunken tales of a "winged snake" that he read about in someone else's book.
- [[The Sage]] will entertain any outlandish theory except that one.
- A certain duke keeps a "captive dragon" in his dungeon to impress and coerce his rivals ([[Puppetmaster]]).
- The townsfolk of [[Shoreside]] treat [[Dragon Reef]] as a tourist attraction and a lucky charm. Nobody thinks it's a real dragon.

## Dragon Motifs Everywhere

Dragons survive as decoration: door handles, jade paperweights, pendants, a jungle tribe's sacred idol. Nobody finds this strange, any more than we find unicorns on a tapestry strange. See [[Treasures/index|Treasures]].

> [!secret]- GM Only
> The disbelief is the point. Two secret orders know the truth, the [[Order of the Orchid]] and the [[Calla Cabal]], and both rely on public ridicule to operate unseen. The Order hides its mission so it isn't mocked for chasing fairy tales. The Cabal hides so the Order can't find it. See [[Campaign Overview]].
