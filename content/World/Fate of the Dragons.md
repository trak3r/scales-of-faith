*What happens when the dragons wake up.*

> [!secret]- GM Only
> ## No Time Has Passed
> - When a dragon is revived, **no time has passed** from its perspective.
> - From its point of view, strange humans have suddenly appeared and claim that its race, thriving mere seconds ago, is practically extinct, and that a *thousand years* have passed.
> - For this reason the [[Calla Cabal]] opens the portal to [[The Plane of Dreams]] *before* reviving a dragon, and brings a previously rescued dragon to help with the transition.
>
> ## Civil War
> - In the Plane of Dreams, the rescued dragons **debate the fate of mankind**.
> - **One faction** wants justice: repay the genocide in kind.
> - **Another faction** recognizes that mankind ultimately saved them from extinction.
> - As the debate escalates, the battle eventually **spills over into the real world**.
