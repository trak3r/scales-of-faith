## Description

- A large, heavy, reptilian eyeball the size of a bowling ball.
- It looks and feels like blown glass, and it's very heavy, as if it were solid glass.
- You could swear the pupil shrinks and grows, almost imperceptibly.

## Effect

When a character holds the orb and concentrates:

- **They see** a bright neon sky with blurry silhouettes flying around.
- **They hear** deep, rumbling voices, too muddled to make out words.
- **They smell** sweet lavender and harsh sulfur burning their nose.
- They feel as though they lack the power to fully use the orb, or perhaps they just aren't the right species.

## Drawbacks

- It travels in a chest, wrapped in a magically trapped cloth that tries to strangle whoever opens it unless a magic word is spoken.
