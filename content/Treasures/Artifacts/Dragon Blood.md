*A tiny vial of dark red sap, kept by a man who says dragons never existed.*

## Description

- A tiny glass vial with a wax-dipped seal.
- The contents are a dark red, viscous, sap-like liquid.
- If opened, it smells acrid and sulfurous.

## Effect

- Rumored to heal a mortal wound if drunk.

## Where Found

> [!secret]- GM Only
> [[The Sage]] always carries it, hidden and protected deep in the folds of his clothing.
