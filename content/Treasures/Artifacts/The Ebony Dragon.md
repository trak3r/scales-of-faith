## Description

- A small ebony statue of a dragon entwining a globe (the world).
- Pure black. The dragon looks sinister.
- It emanates pure evil. No checks or rolls are needed to sense that.
- It's uncharacteristically heavy for its size, and takes considerable strength to carry.

## Effect

- Anything that dies in the room where it rests is raised as undead.

## Drawbacks

- It can, in theory, be removed. But it inflicts *feeblemind* on anything that stays close to it, no matter how well it's wrapped and shielded.
- Pack animals refuse to carry it, and will batter themselves to death if forced.

## Where Found

- The northern alcove of the master's chamber in [[The Crypt]].
- [[The Sage]] sends the party to fetch it.
