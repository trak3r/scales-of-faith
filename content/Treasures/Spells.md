*Dragon magic, and the Order's answer to it.*

How characters learn new spells here: through a scroll (contents unknown).

## Dragon's Direction

- The caster gets a vague sense of the direction and distance of the closest stone dragon. See [[The Stone Dragons]].

## Dragon's Blessing

- A small bonus to all rolls, depending on your system and the GM's discretion.
- Wears off after a day or a long rest, unless renewed.
- Source: the [[Dragon Figurine]], if treated well.

## Dragon's Curse

- A small penalty to all rolls, depending on your system and the GM's discretion.
- Wears off after a day or a long rest, unless renewed.
- Source: the [[Dragon Figurine]], if mistreated.

## Dragon's Fury

- For a few rounds of combat, attacks and spells are considerably more potent.
- Source: the [[Dragon Head Pendant]].

## Guarded Slumber

- The caster and companions can't be surprised during a long rest.
- They can still be interrupted or attacked, but they won't be caught off guard.
- It works like an early-warning system plus a shot of adrenaline, so the characters are instantly ready for action.
- Members of the [[Order of the Orchid]] *always* include it in their nightly prayers. See [[Knight of the Order]].

## Holy Word of Power

- Used by [[Knight of the Order|knights of the Order]] to smash stone dragons.
