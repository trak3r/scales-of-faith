*A gem that looks like it's reflecting water when held up to the light.*

## Description

- One of the gems in Fintooth's hoard looks as if it's reflecting water when held up to the light.

## Effect

- Grants the holder *water breathing* and a swim speed.
- [[Jerem]] can set it into an amulet.

## Drawbacks

- If it's worn openly, sailors in [[Sea Voyage]] will recognize it.

## Where Found

- [[Fintooth]]'s hoard, in [[Fishy Business]].
