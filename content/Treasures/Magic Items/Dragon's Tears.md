*A few milky drops that can cure almost anything.*

## Description

- A milky white potion.
- Usually found in very small, easily concealed vials of just a few drops.
- Extremely rare and extremely valuable.

## Effect

- A single drop on the tongue gives major healing, including curing diseases and neutralizing poisons.
