*Impossibly light, opalescent, and unscratchable.*

## Description

- A large shield so light it seems inconceivable it could block the weakest attack. But it does.
- The front is opalescent, a shimmering rainbow like oil on water.
- The surface can't be scratched.
- Unique.

## Effect

- Deflects 10% of magical elemental damage (fireballs, chain lightning, frost blasts, and so on).

## Where Found

- Unplaced.
