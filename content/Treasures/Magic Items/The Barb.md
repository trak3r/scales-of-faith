*A barbed spear that won't come out until its victim is dead.*

## Description

- An enchanted barbed spear.
- A family heirloom of a pauper carpenter in [[Stonebridge]].

## Effect

- +1 spear.
- Once it pierces a living creature, The Barb can't be removed until the creature is dead.

## Drawbacks

- The carpenter can't offer a reward for its return, and will immediately sell it.

## Where Found

- Stuck in the flank of the [[Bulette]], in [[Gordon's Dragon Experience]].
