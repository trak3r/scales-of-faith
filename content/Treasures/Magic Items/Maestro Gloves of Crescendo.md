*Patience, rewarded.*

## Effect

For every round of combat in which the wearer could attack and doesn't, the *following* turn gains a bonus to attack and damage. The effect builds for up to three turns:

| Turns skipped | Bonus |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | +2 attack, double damage |
| 2 | +4 attack with advantage, triple damage |
| 3 | +8 attack with advantage, quadruple damage |

## Drawbacks

- Once per combat encounter.
