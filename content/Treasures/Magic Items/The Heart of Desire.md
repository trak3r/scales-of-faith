*A large ruby amulet that makes the wearer more charming, and more of a target.*

## Description

- A large ruby amulet on a thick golden chain.

## Effect

- Worn openly, it grants +1 charisma.

## Drawbacks

- Worn openly, it also attracts the attention of greedy cutthroats.

## Where Found

- Worn by the zombie fourth wife in [[The Crypt]].
