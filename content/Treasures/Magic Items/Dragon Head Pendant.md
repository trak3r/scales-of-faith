*A dragon's head with ruby eyes that burn when it's used.*

## Description

- A dragon's head with ruby eyes, much like a witcher's medallion.
- Unique.

## Effect

- Once per day, the wearer can invoke *Dragon's Fury*. See [[Spells]].
- The ruby eyes burn intensely while it's in use.

## Drawbacks

- Afterwards, it's uncomfortably cold against bare skin for an hour.
