*Fishermen have broken an old pact with the naiads upriver. The mayor wants it fixed, one way or another.*

## Hooks

- After a poor spawning season, the town's fishermen are venturing too far upriver and encroaching on a waterfall pool occupied by a family of naiads, **violating a pact of peace** with the town.
- [[Mayor Midge]] is hiring adventurers to smooth relations with the naiads, or to "solve" the problem with indiscretion.
- She mentions a druid somewhere in the surrounding woods who might help, but nobody knows how to find him ([[Truman the Druid]]).
- She offers an **extra 10 gp to the families** of any adventurers who don't survive, as long as the quest succeeds.
