*The river is rising, two lumberjacks are missing, and something with very big teeth is building downstream.*

## Hooks

- The river level is rising, and people suspect an obstruction downstream.
- A fallen tree has been reported.
- Two lumberjacks volunteered to handle it. **They haven't returned.**

## Scenes

### The Obstruction

- The tree appears to have fallen naturally: a splintered trunk, no axe or tooth marks.
- A lot of debris is collecting behind it and slowing the water.

### Ambush

- **Dire beavers** attack by surprise while the party investigates or tries to clear the obstruction. See [[Dire Beasts]].

## Aftermath

- Once the beavers are defeated, the silhouette of a robed figure with a large gnarled staff is seen in the trees on the far bank.
- If asked, townsfolk speculate it was [[Truman the Druid]].

## Secrets

> [!secret]- GM Only
> Truman watches the whole fight and doesn't intervene.
