*Barrels of fish keep vanishing. The trail leads into the sewers, to a shark-man on a driftwood throne.*

## Hooks

- [[Gill Herring]], master of the Fishermen's Guild, has hired [[Shamrock Loams]] to find out what's causing the missing supplies. Shamrock hires the party.

## Prep

> [!prep]
> **Maps**: Sewer
> **Tokens**: Fintooth, minions

> [!map] Map placeholder: Stonebridge Sewers
> Battle map, Dungeondraft. See `maps/stonebridge-sewers/`.

## Scenes

### Into the Sewers

- The trail leads to [[The Sewers]], with its foul water and gas, rusted grates, pit traps and lurking creatures.
- [[Fintooth's Minions]] patrol and guard corners, intersections and halls.
- [[Turd the Troll]] lets the party past the main entrance under the bridge, for a treat.

### The Driftwood Throne

- [[Fintooth]], a recluse shark-man, holds court from his throne of driftwood.
- He's building a small army of minions and sending them on small raids for barrels of fish. There are so many sewer entrances that they can reach the entire town.
- **If he has to negotiate for his life**, he offers a map to the untold treasures of [[Dragon Reef]].

## Encounters

- [[Fintooth's Minions]].
- Optional sewer horrors: see [[Creatures of the Sewers]].

## Treasure

- [[Fintooth's Trident]].
- **Fintooth's hoard**, in a compartment under the seat of the driftwood throne: jewelry, gems and coins paid as tribute by the minions, and a treasure map, perhaps to [[Dragon Reef]].
- The [[Sapphire of the Sea]].

## Aftermath

- Fintooth's map is a lead into [[Raising the Reef]].

## Secrets

> [!secret]- GM Only
> - Fintooth fled the sea after making enemies, swam up the river, and found the town's sewer outlet.
> - In his open-sea days he visited [[Dragon Reef]]. This is the adventure's link to the main quest.
