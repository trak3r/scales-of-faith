*A duke claims to have imprisoned a dragon. Elites and dignitaries come from far and wide to consult it.*

## Hooks

- A duke claims to have **imprisoned a dragon**, attracting the attention of elites and neighboring dignitaries.

## Prep

> [!map] Map placeholder: The Duke's Amphitheater
> Battle map, Dungeondraft. Underground amphitheater, caged stage, distant balcony. See `maps/dukes-amphitheater/`.

## Scenes

### The Amphitheater

See [[The Duke's Keep]].

- An enormous amphitheater beneath the keep, with the dragon behind floor-to-ceiling iron bars.
- Giant flaming braziers keep the room unpleasantly hot.

### The Balcony

- Visitors are **disarmed**.
- The balcony is an **anti-magic zone**.
- The entry is a **dispel magic portal** that disenchants any magic item carried through it. The characters are warned about this! If they sneak something in and it gets destroyed, they made their choice.

## Secrets

> [!secret]- GM Only
> - **It's an elaborate puppet** with a dash of illusion magic, worked by a team of gnomes. Its booming voice is an enchanted megaphone.
> - The duke uses the dragon as a **false oracle** to deceive and coerce allies and enemies.
