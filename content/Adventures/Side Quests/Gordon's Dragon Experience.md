*A self-titled dragonologist leads a paid field trip to see an invisible dragon. Something is definitely there.*

## Hooks

- [[Gordon the Dragonologist]] advertises with **posters** around town.
- He offers consultations on dragons for a fee, and a **"dragon experience" field trip**.

## Prep

> [!map] Map placeholder: The Bulette Grounds
> Battle map, Dungeondraft. See `maps/bulette-grounds/`.

## Scenes

### The Clearing

- Gordon leads the party to a clearing in the woods where, he says, an invisible dragon lives: [[The Bulette Grounds]].
- It's close to the [[Centaur Clan]]'s territory, so the two can overlap.
- A character with tracking skills can confirm that a **large creature** has been here recently: the tall grass is trampled, and there are large footprints in loose sand, too blurred to identify.

### The Dragon Arrives

- A **breeze** starts to blow, and the **ground begins to rumble**.
- Gordon proclaims: **the dragon is here!**

## Encounters

- It's actually a [[Bulette]] that has decided to stop tolerating trespassers.
- If the party needs rescuing and the GM is feeling charitable, the centaurs might arrive to help.
- (Optional) The bulette's burrows can be explored as a cave system.

## Treasure

- [[The Barb]] is stuck in the bulette's flank. (Or substitute a weapon reward that suits the party.)

## Aftermath

- Gordon may or may not accept that his dragon was a bulette.
