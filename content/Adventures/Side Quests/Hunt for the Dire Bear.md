*A leatherworker wants a pelt, the couriers want their routes back, and a cave full of bones holds the answer.*

## Hooks

- [[Hyde]] of [[Hyde's Leatherworks]] needs a dire bear pelt to make a cloak for a local noble.
- The [[Celtic Courier]] has lost delivery animals on one route to something big.
- If the party has no hunter or tracker, they'll need to hire one.
- If the party is on good terms with the [[Courier Druids]], the druids lend a small **wolf companion** to lead them to the bear. The wolf won't fight for or with the party, only to defend itself.

## Scenes

### Travel

Classic forest travel encounters: wolves, wild elves, a giant eagle, bandits. See [[Random Encounters]].

### The Cave

[[Dire Bear Cave]]: the entrance is littered with bones, much like the killer rabbit's lair in *Monty Python and the Holy Grail*.

- **By day**, there's a good chance the bear is asleep. The chance drops if the party is noisy, wearing heavy clanking armor, or the wolf starts growling, barking or howling. The bear won't leave the cave in daylight unless it must. It waits for attackers to come to it.
- **By night**, there's a good chance the bear is out of the cave. It attacks the party from the woods, or waits for them to go inside and then blocks the entrance.

## Encounters

- A dire bear. See [[Dire Beasts]].

## Treasure

- Among the litter of corpses: plenty of coin, some gems, perhaps a valuable item at the GM's discretion.
- A *lot* of **undelivered messages and packages** from the [[Celtic Courier]]'s lost animals.
- **The pelt:** if the party manages *not* to eviscerate the hide, Hyde pays for it. He makes 20 gp on the finished cloak, so his offer depends on how much profit he'll give up and the quality of the pelt, probably around 6 gp.
- **The meat:** [[Ground and Pound]] pays about 2 gp.
