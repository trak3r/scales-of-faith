*The campaign opens in the heat of the moment: "This is a robbery."*

## Hooks

- This is the **opening session**. No setup needed: the party is already in [[The Slattern Tavern]] when it happens.
- (Optional) It can be a wagon robbery instead, if that suits the party better.

## Prep

> [!prep]
> **Maps**: Tavern
> **Tokens**: Cassidy, Butch, Sundancer, Kitt, Seraphina, Brock, extra patrons

> [!map] Map placeholder: The Slattern Tavern
> Battle map, Dungeondraft. See `maps/the-slattern-tavern/`.

## Scenes

### Setup

- Let the players place their characters anywhere on the tavern map.
- Before any role-play begins, an NPC antagonist announces: **"This is a robbery."**

### The Robbery

- The bandits are **neutral good thieves, not murderers**.
- They defend themselves and try to flee if things turn sour.
- **They will not kill.**

## Encounters

### The Bandits

| Bandit | Description | Tactics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cassidy** | Ringleader. A handsome, charismatic human bard. | Stands by the bar and announces the robbery. Supports his companions with bardic magic. |
| **Butch** | A brawny half-orc barbarian. | Blocks the door. Grapples and subdues anyone who doesn't comply. |
| **Sundancer** | A nimble, svelte elven thief. | Walks the dining room with a sack, imploring victims to hand over coin and jewelry. |
| **Kitt** | A wildcard: a hooded mage sitting in the shadows of a back corner. | Pretends to be one of the victims. Uses *sleep*, *charm* and *hold person* if things get out of hand. |

## Aftermath

- [[Seraphina]] and the regulars, [[Brock Blusterhaus]] among them, now know the party's faces.
- The session ends with the party established in [[Stonebridge]]. Lead into [[The First Last Dragon]].
