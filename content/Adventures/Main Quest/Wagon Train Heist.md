*Steal a rumored dragon artifact from an armored wagon without hurting the innocent caravan around it.*

## Hooks

- Some **dragon-related artifact** is *rumored* to be traveling with a particular merchant caravan.
- The party must steal it **without disturbing the rest of the caravan**, whose merchants are innocent bystanders.

## Scenes

### The Wagon

- The period equivalent of an **armored truck**: thick wooden walls; iron bands, hinges and locks; pulled by two large draft horses.
- **The guards don't have the key.** Only the destination does.
- Inside is another heavily built, locked chest.

### The Guards

- Well armed, armored and trained: plate mail, round shields, longswords, medium warhorses.
- They are not evil and should not be murdered (by a lawful party).
- The driver carries a heavy crossbow.
- All the horses are well trained and very hard to spook.

### The Camp

- When the caravan stops for the night, the guarded wagon **isolates itself** from the rest of the group.
- The guards set **tripwires** of noisy empty cans and chimes around their camp.
- **One guard is always awake** on watch, pacing the perimeter. At the slightest suspicion he alerts the rest with a sharp whistle, without hesitation.
- **One guard sleeps on top of the wagon** with the crossbow, and gets a surprise attack if the party doesn't know he's there.
- For more challenge, give the guards a vicious dog.
- The other merchants won't help or interfere, as long as their own wagons aren't threatened.

### The Chest

- **Magically too heavy** to lift or even drag. Dispelling it is a very difficult check, and a critical success also dispels the cloth inside.
- The lock is trapped with a **poison needle**.
- **Poison gas** releases when the lid opens unless particular pressure points are pressed. Constitution check or choke to death.
- The contents are wrapped in a **magically trapped cloth** that tries to strangle the opener unless a magic word is spoken.
