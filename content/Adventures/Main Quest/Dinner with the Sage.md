*A mysterious invitation, a private dining room, and an exchange of stories.*

## Hooks

- On returning to town after [[The First Last Dragon]], the party receives a **mysterious invitation to dinner**.

## Prep

> [!prep]
> **Maps**: Tavern (private dining room)
> **Tokens**: The Sage, Seraphina, serving girl

## Scenes

### The Private Room

- At [[The Slattern Tavern]], the party is escorted to a private dining room, garishly decorated with a hearth, sconces and mounted trophies of deer, boar and one exotic lion.
- Their host, [[The Sage]], makes introductions and proposes an **exchange of stories**.

### Their Story

- He asks them to tell the story of their quest with the black knight.
- If the party gets greedy, he spills a small sack of **precious gems** onto the table.
- He points out that the knight's rush to reach the statue implies a **race with another party**.

### His Story

In return, he tells them:

- He has been curious about the [[Order of the Orchid]] for decades.
- He once tracked a troupe into a dense forest, where they found and destroyed an out-of-place, enormous boulder covered in vines and moss.
- After they left, he spent days searching the rubble and realized it had been an ancient, weather-worn **statue of a dragon**.

What he wants to discover:

- Where do these statues come from?
- Why does the Order want to destroy them?
- Who is the mystery organization racing the Order?

### Ground Rules

- The Sage deflects any personal questions.
- He **vehemently ridicules** any suggestion that dragons actually exist: *"I will entertain a plethora of outlandish theories, but supposing the existence of a creature from children's fairy tales is a bit too far."*

### The Serving Girl (optional)

- A serving girl overhears the conversation and asks whether there's a reward for information.
- The Sage offers her the pick of one gem from his sack.
- She tells them about a regular who gets drunk and tells outlandish stories, [[Brock Blusterhaus]]. One of his stories involves the statue of a **winged snake**, which she reckons is a dragon.
- This leads to [[Journey to U'gachaka]].

## Aftermath

- The party has a patron who pays for stories, and a first hint that someone else is racing the Order.
