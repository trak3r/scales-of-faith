*A secretive organization has bought the right to raise Shoreside's famous reef, and everybody wants a piece of what happens next.*

## Hooks

- **Leads:** [[The Sage]]; [[Fintooth]]'s treasure map from [[Fishy Business]]; or the [[Calla Cabal]] or [[Order of the Orchid]], if the party has pledged loyalty.
- In [[Shoreside]], a suspicious captain is looking to hire **independent mercenaries** to cover himself during the operation.

## Prep

> [!prep]
> **Maps**: Shoreside town, Tavern/Inn, Ship deck, Barge, Warehouse, Reef
> **Tokens**: Townsfolk, Cabal mages, Order knights, Sahuagin, Chuul, Giant Oyster

## Scenes

### Gossip and Investigation

- [[Dragon Reef]] is a tourist attraction, a good-luck token for sailors and fishermen, and a living for pearl divers.
- A mysterious organization with "deep pockets" has made a "sizable donation" to the town for the rights to "raise the reef."
- **Townsfolk who profit from the reef are not happy.**
- Lodging: [[The Prawn Bucket]] or the [[Seaview Inn]].

### Weather

Roll for weather each day and night (see [[Weather]]). The **barge captain refuses to sail unless the seas are calm**. Some coin might change his mind, but he'll be short-handed, since half his crew value their lives.

### The Raising

- The Cabal raises the statue with a **barge**, using mages and **levitation** magic.
- Creatures attack the barge and interfere (see Encounters).
- The statue is taken to a large **warehouse**.

### The Night Raid

- That same night, the [[Order of the Orchid]] **ambushes the warehouse** to destroy the statue.
- Depending on the party's loyalties, they must stop the Order or help it.

## Encounters

See [[Creatures of the Reef]].

- **Sahuagin** try to board the barge, perhaps even attacking during the voyage. They have shark telepathy and can ride sharks!
- **Chuul**, a lobster-like tentacled horror.
- **Giant oyster**, which tries to grab characters and drown them.

## Treasure

- **Sahuagin:** miscellaneous baubles (rings, gems, coins). One wears a *belt of endurance*.
- **Chuul:** gut it to find 200 gp worth of jewelry, gems and coins, including a *ring of water walking*.
- **Giant oyster:** valuable mother-of-pearl, and a large yellow pearl worth 200 gp.

## Aftermath

- Perhaps leading to a voyage: [[Journey to U'gachaka]].
