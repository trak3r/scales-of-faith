*Break into the opposing order's hidden office and steal its secrets.*

## Hooks

- In a town too small for a formal temple, the **opposing order** works out of a small, nondescript building and keeps a low profile.
- The party is hired to **break in and collect information**.
- (Optional) The building might be the office of [[Shamrock Loams]] ([[Shamrock Investigations]]).

## Prep

> [!map] Map placeholder: The Hidden Office
> Battle map, Dungeondraft. A small, nondescript building. See `maps/hidden-office/`.

## Scenes

### Getting In

- The building is well protected with magical and physical **traps and alarms**.
- Failing a trap brings its normal physical punishment.
- **Tripping an alarm** brings enemies within a few turns.

### What They Find

- **Crude, vague maps** to potential dragon and artifact locations, which can lead to other adventures in the campaign.
- **Letters** to and from the church's high priests, coordinating operations in a pseudo-code of ambiguous terms, so intercepted messages wouldn't seem important.
- Perhaps one letter **exposes the party's contact as a double agent**.

## Treasure

If the room is searched thoroughly:

- [[Dragon Compass]]: points to dragon-related "stuff."
- [[Inkwell of Inspiration]]: writings are more persuasive.
- Random spell scrolls tailored to the party's classes.
- A **magically hidden and locked safe** holding considerable coin and gems. It can be carried off with considerable strength.

## Secrets

> [!secret]- GM Only
> "The opposing order" depends on the party's loyalties: the Order's office if they lean Cabal, and the Cabal's if they lean Order. See [[Faction Loyalty]].
