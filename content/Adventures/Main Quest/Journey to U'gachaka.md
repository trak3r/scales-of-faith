*A drunk's borrowed story, a book read from inside a cage, and weeks of travel to a jungle idol.*

## Hooks

- [[Brock Blusterhaus]] tells a tipsy tavern tale of a **winged snake** statue worshipped by a jungle tribe.
- He actually read it in an old book by a more renowned adventurer: *Ahiro's Journey*, by Ahiro Tamagotchi. Most of his tales are "borrowed" from books.
- The serving girl at [[The Slattern Tavern]] may point the party his way ([[Dinner with the Sage]]).

## Scenes

### The Bookstore

- The bookstore won't sell a copy, but the proprietor lets someone read his personal copy from **the cage**.
- To prevent theft, the reader is **locked in a cage**, then handed the book from the opposite wall.
- The book must be returned intact for the reader to be released.
- It gives hints at the general location, but nothing concrete.
- The book is so old there's no telling whether the tribe or the statue still exist.

### Travel

- The jungle is very far away. Getting there takes **weeks** of side adventures.
- **By ship**, around the horn: faster, but a longer distance.
- **Overland**: more direct and shorter, but slower.
- The [[Order of the Orchid]] and the [[Calla Cabal]] catch wind and pursue the party hotly.

### The Tribe

- The party must **hire an interpreter**. The tribe speaks a unique language.
- The tribe are courteous hosts, tolerant up to a point.
- The statue is so covered in wreaths and offerings that its legs are hidden. It's easy to see why it was mistaken for a winged snake.
- They **will not tolerate shenanigans** around their sacred statue.

### The Orders Arrive

- **If the Order arrives** and tries to destroy the statue, the tribe blames the party and attacks, unless the party helps defend it.
- **If the Cabal arrives** and makes the statue disappear, the tribe blames the party for their arrival and attacks.

## Encounters

See [[Jungle Perils]] and [[Random Encounters]].

- A hostile tribe with poison-tipped weapons, maybe cannibals.
- Giant leeches, killer vines, carnivorous plants.
- A swarm of tiny, super-fast monkeys that leap onto characters and steal items, sheathed weapons, even unfasten armor.
- A falling net; a spike trap.

## Treasure

- A healing natural spring where the party can fill their waterskins.
- The remains of another party, with one random valuable item.
