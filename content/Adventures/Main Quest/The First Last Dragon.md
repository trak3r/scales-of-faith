*The party escorts a lone holy knight to a forgotten cavern, where he shatters a statue for no reason anyone will explain.*

## Hooks

- A priest or paladin in the party is **summoned to the local temple** by the high priest.
- The high priest introduces a lone [[Knight of the Order]], [[The Black Knight]].
- The knight must complete a **time-sensitive holy quest**, and his companions have been delayed, so he needs an impromptu fellowship.
- The assignment is a matter of **goodwill** between the player's church and the [[Order of the Orchid]].

## Scenes

### Travel

- The knight has hired [[Matty Mooseburgher]] to handle the camp.
- **The first night**, the knight tells the party, "No guards are necessary while traveling with me." Indeed, there are no mid-night random encounters (*Guarded Slumber*, see [[Spells]]).
- **Each night** the knight rereads an ancient scroll telling the tale of a dragon that lived in the destination cavern. He won't share it and deflects any questions.
- He then reads several pages of his holy book before bed.
- He packs the scroll and the book securely in the knapsack he uses as a pillow. Stealing a peek would take miraculous skill.
- He is inexplicably **always awake before** the rest of the party.

### Ambush (optional)

Once the party leaves the main road for a game trail, a band of [[Wild Wood Elves]] ambushes them.

### The Cavern

- Optional filler: a bugbear, a roper, a cave-in, a carrion crawler, or a patrol of orcs and goblins.
- Eventually the party reaches [[The Dragon Cavern]] and **the statue**: massive, exquisitely detailed, a little weather-worn and lichen-covered, but otherwise in remarkable shape.
- The knight lays out candles in a ritual pattern and asks the party for privacy to meditate.
- **Without warning**, he utters a *Holy Word of Power* and smashes the statue with his mighty maul.

### The Return

- The [[Calla Cabal]], realizing it arrived too late, has set a **trap** for its rivals: a tree felled across the path and warded with magic.
  - A lightning shock that chains to every character.
  - Paralysis and summoned creatures.
- If questioned, the knight admits the trap was meant for him and that he knows who set it, but gives no details.
