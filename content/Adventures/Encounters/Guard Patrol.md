*Mounted guards hunting an assassin, and not inclined to believe the party.*

## Hooks

- A random road-travel encounter, most likely between [[Shoreside]] and [[Stonebridge]].

## Scenes

### The Patrol

- A patrol of mounted guards from the next town: a captain, two officers and a cleric.
- They are looking for the [[Elven Assassin]].
- If the party has her horse, they recognize it, but play coy until they've heard the party's story.
- They **won't believe** any claim to have defeated or killed her.
- If the party tells outlandish lies, the captain asks the cleric to cast *zone of truth*.
- They mock the party for missing out on an exorbitant bounty.
- They ask where the party last saw her and which way she was headed.
