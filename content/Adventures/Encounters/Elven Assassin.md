*A lone young elf rides past, strumming a lute. There's blood on her horse's leg.*

## Hooks

- A random road-travel encounter.

## Prep

> [!prep]
> **Maps**: Forest road, or a fork in the road
> **Tokens**: Female elf, horse

> [!map] Map placeholder: Forest Road
> Battle map, Dungeondraft. See `maps/forest-road/`.

## Scenes

### The Rider

- The party passes a lone young female elf on horseback.
- She is strumming a lute and humming, casting a **calming** effect.
- She won't engage. She just rides by, unless they speak to her.
- A **hard perception check** spots a splatter of blood on her horse's hind leg.
- If the party mentions the blood, she sighs: *"Aw, and I was hoping to let you all live."*

### The Fight

- She opens by acrobatically leaping off her horse and vanishing into the roadside trees.
- As a free action, she whistles commands to her horse, which kicks or charges the nearest unmounted opponent.
- She wields a short bow and two curved daggers.
- As far as possible, she stays in the treetops and rains arrows down on the party, while her horse wreaks havoc on the ground.

## Aftermath

- See [[Guard Patrol]]: the next town's guards are hunting her.

## Secrets

> [!secret]- GM Only
> Her appearance: well hidden high in a tall, leafy tree, in dark green leather armor, olive-skinned, dirty-faced, sinister and dangerous.
