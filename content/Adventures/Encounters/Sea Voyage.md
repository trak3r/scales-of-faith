*A sea captain, a dragon-shaped cliff, and everything that can go wrong at sea.*

## Hooks

- A ship captain tells of a **cliffside rock outcrop that resembles a dragon**.
- The party has to time the tide to approach it. The rocks are slippery with sea spray and seaweed.
- A smugglers' cave is nearby.

> [!note]
> This overlaps with [[Raising the Reef]], which has more potential. Use it as voyage seeds, for example on the way to [[U'gachaka]].

## Scenes

### The Ship

- **Captain Jean Finch** commands the vessel.
- An unusual crew member, a **drow**, always stays below decks to avoid the sun, coming up only at night or under thick cloud.

### Seasickness

- Constitution or dexterity checks for seasickness. Failure means −2 on rolls until the character has spent 24 hours on dry land.

## Encounters

Pick or roll (see [[Random Encounters]]):

- pirates
- mermaids
- sirens
- a sea serpent
- a naga
- a maelstrom or whirlpool
- a ghost ship
