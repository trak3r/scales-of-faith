*Every full moon, the town leatherworker buys a slab of meat and disappears into the sewers.*

## Hooks

- [[Shamrock Loams]] has noticed [[Hyde]]'s suspicious behavior and may ask the party to look into it.

## Scenes

### The Purchase

- On the night of the full moon, Hyde buys a slab of meat from [[Ground and Pound]].

### The Cell

- He locks himself in a cell in [[The Sewers]] beneath the town.

## Secrets

> [!secret]- GM Only
> - Hyde is a **were-rat**. He does this to protect the townsfolk from his violent alter ego.
> - If discovered in his cell, he is surrounded by small sewer rats, like a pied piper.
