*Another adventuring party comes back to town, as undead.*

## Hooks

- Another adventuring party set out to investigate [[The Crypt]] and hasn't been seen since.
- Then they come back.

## Scenes

### The Attack

- The lost party returns to [[Stonebridge]] as a random mix of skeletons and zombies, scaled to the characters' level.
- Some townsfolk can help if needed, such as [[Bridgette Brightforge]].

## Encounters

The victims are still geared as standard adventurers:

| Former adventurer | Gear |
| --- | --- |
| Ranger | Bow |
| Fighter | Sword, shield, armor |
| Fighter | Quarterstaff |
| Mage | Green robe and pointed hat |
| Thief | Purple cape, daggers. (Optional) She died wearing a *cloak of invisibility* and is still invisible. |
| Halfling fighter | Club, horned helm |

## Treasure

- A few coins, but no gems or valuable jewelry.
- Perhaps some minor magic items.
- Perhaps a **crudely drawn partial map** of the first few rooms of [[The Crypt]].

## Aftermath

- A trigger to lead into [[The Crypt]].
- [[The Sage]] may also take an interest.

## Secrets

> [!secret]- GM Only
> - This may be the party that [[Matty Mooseburgher]] waited for for three days and three nights before going home alone.
> - The lineup is a nod to a classic 1980s fantasy cartoon. Rename freely.
