*A traveling troupe brings jousting, wrestling-style rivalries and rigged contests to town.*

## Hooks

- A traveling troupe puts on a "Medieval Times" show for real, with a dash of pro-wrestling flair.
- **Fliers** go up around town announcing the event and adding "flavor" to the fake rivalry between heroes and heels.
- In the evenings, the show's **heroes and heels hang out in the taverns**, drumming up fan bases.
- As the event approaches, fans wear their "team's" colors and banners.
- [[Mayor Midge]] makes an appearance.

**Name alternatives:** "Thymes" instead of "Times," with a sprig of the herb as the troupe's symbol and signage. Or "Contemporary" or "Present" instead of "Modern."

## Scenes

### The Contests

- **Jousting.** Few players will have the mount and armor to compete, so this is mostly a spectator and betting event.
- **Sparring.** Non-lethal combat with blunted weapons, with a twist: one hand tied behind the back, fighting in a pit of deep mud, or opponents' ankles joined by a short chain.
- **Archery.** Needs something cleverer than a target.
- **Feats of strength.** Throw something big and heavy. Move something from point A to point B. Tug-of-war with a beast.
- **Magic.** Put the most people in a crowd to sleep. Knock over the largest object. Charm a resistant volunteer. Detect the magic items in a pile of random junk.

### Opponents

- For each event, the GM invents one or two shallow townspeople (no stat block, no backstory) with skills comparable to the participating characters.
- A good opportunity to introduce an important NPC for a later part of the campaign.

## Encounters

### The Troupe

The main "story" of the show is David versus Goliath: an underdog overcoming adversity.

**Gluk Golgan**, the "villain" heel
- *See:* an enormous half-orc (more like 99% orc).
- *Smell:* lilac and lavender.
- *Hear:* a deep, rumbling voice.
- He plays the ruthless villain, but he's really a tender, caring, polite softie.

**Andre the Halfling**, the "hero"
- *See:* short, in a short sleeveless tunic that shows off his rippling muscles.
- *Smell:* baby oil and sweat.
- *Hear:* talks fast, like a used-car salesman.
- He plays the humble hero, thinks he's the star of the show, and is a prick.

**Lady Valora**, the troupe's cleric
- *See:* strikingly attractive, and nimble for her age.
- *Smell:* chalk and sap.
- *Hear:* a mellifluous voice with a hint of rasp.
- A high-level cleric who travels with the troupe to make sure nobody is killed or maimed. In her downtime she volunteers at the host town's temple or hospital. She might accompany the party on any adventure that doesn't take her too far from her duty to the troupe.

**Vinny McMahem**, ringleader and manager
- *See:* short and rotund, with an elaborate mustache.
- *Smell:* pipe smoke and flowery cologne.
- *Hear:* a high-pitched, grainy voice, like an old-timey radio announcer.
- An all-around good guy who treats his people well and wants everyone to have fun.

**Everybody else** (for the GM to flesh out):
- An illusionist who performs impressive but impractical spells.
- **Horace Copes**, an oracle who tells fortunes.
- An accountant who handles ticket sales and salaries.
- A group of *seven* hard-working dwarves who build the bleachers and other structures at each stop. They whistle while they work.
- A doe-eyed stable boy who tends the horses and wagons and dreams of one day being part of the show.

## Treasure

This is a traveling troupe in a small backwoods town, so the coin prizes are small. But some prizes that *look* like worthless trinkets are actually valuable:

- A "pretty scroll of gibberish" turns out to be an ancient elven spell, encoded directions to buried treasure, or a clue leading to another chapter of the campaign.
- A rusty old blade, cleaned and honed, turns out to be enchanted.
- Some cheap glass costume jewelry turns out to be magical.
