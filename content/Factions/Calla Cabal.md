*An organization so secret that practically nobody knows it exists.*

## History

> [!secret]- GM Only
> - A **defector** from the [[Order of the Orchid]] came to believe that dragons might have been critical to the **balance of nature** in the world.
> - They recruited like-minded people and founded the Cabal.

## Goals

> [!secret]- GM Only
> - Find the few remaining [[The Stone Dragons|stone dragons]] **before the Order** does.
> - **Revive** them and send them to [[The Plane of Dreams]] until the world is safe for their return.
> - Ease each dragon's shock by opening the Dream portal *before* the revival, with a previously rescued dragon present ([[Fate of the Dragons]]).

## Presence and Symbols

- The Cabal is super secretive. Practically nobody outside the organization knows it exists.
- Its symbol only ever appears as a **subtle stencil or obfuscated tattoo**.
- Members dress as nondescript townspeople and blend easily into crowds.
- Most members are **druids or rangers**.

## Members

> [!secret]- GM Only
> - Cabal agents lodge at the [[Seaview Inn]] in Shoreside during [[Raising the Reef]].
> - It has "deep pockets," enough to make a sizable donation to Shoreside for the rights to "raise the reef."
> - Its mages raise statues with barges and levitation magic, and lay magical traps (lightning wards, paralysis, summoned creatures) for rivals.

## Secrets

> [!secret]- GM Only
> - The Cabal wants the [[Dragon Claw Daggers]] as a possible clue to another dragon's location ([[Royal Auction]]).
> - When it reaches a statue too late, it sets traps for the Order on the road home ([[The First Last Dragon]]).

## Relationships

- [[Order of the Orchid]]: sworn rivals. The Order suspects the Cabal exists but can't prove it.
- [[The Sage]]: suspects that *someone* is racing the Order, and assumes they must be nefarious.
- [[Faction Loyalty]]: how a character joins.
