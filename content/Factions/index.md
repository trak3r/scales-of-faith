## The Two Orders

The campaign turns on two secret organizations racing for the same statues.

- [[Order of the Orchid]]: black-armored paladins who smash stone dragons in The Savior's name.
- [[Calla Cabal]]: druids and rangers who revive the dragons and hide them in the Plane of Dreams.
- [[Faction Loyalty]]: how the party earns either side's trust, and the tattoo that marks them.

## Other Groups

- [[Centaur Clan]]: neutral neighbors in the valley beyond the bulette grounds.
- [[Courier Druids]]: the elves and half-elves behind Stonebridge's animal delivery service.
