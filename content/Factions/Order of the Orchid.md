*A disciplined church of black-armored paladins that most folk dismiss as a quirky cult.*

## History

To the public, the Order is one church among many, devoted to a holy book about an ancient war and a wizard called [[The Savior]]. See [[The Founding of the Order]].

## Goals

> [!secret]- GM Only
> - **Find and destroy every stone dragon** before anyone can study or revive one. See [[The Purge]].
> - Keep that mission secret. The world doesn't believe in dragons, and an order that smashes "statues" of fairy-tale beasts would be laughed at or, worse, would lend credence to the [[Calla Cabal]].
> - Uncover and stop the Cabal. The Order *suspects* the Cabal exists but has no hard evidence yet. That likely changes early in the campaign, once the party is caught between them.

## Presence and Symbols

- Members **always wear black**: robes, tunics, black-enameled armor, all emblazoned with the Order's symbol.
- They are militant, disciplined and loyal. They are not easily fooled or riled to anger.
- Other churches treat the Order as an equal. Most common folk consider it a quirky cult.
- In towns too small for a proper temple, the Order works out of small, nondescript buildings and keeps a low profile ([[Espionage]]).

## Members

- [[Knight of the Order]]: the standard paladin, with a two-handed weapon, the holy book and a scroll of nearby lairs.
- [[The Black Knight]]: the lone knight the party escorts in [[The First Last Dragon]].
- The Order builds goodwill with other churches. It's through one such arrangement that a party priest or paladin is first introduced to a knight.

## Secrets

> [!secret]- GM Only
> - Each knight carries **historical records** (scrolls) of nearby dragon lairs and a **Holy Word of Power** for smashing stone dragons.
> - Knights always include *Guarded Slumber* in their nightly prayers, so they are never caught off guard while camping. See [[Spells]].
> - The Order conscripted gnome inventors to build a contraption for crossing the [[Lake of Fire]]. Several failed attempts litter its shores.
> - The Order has no interest in obvious fakes like [[The Excavation]].

## Relationships

- [[Calla Cabal]]: bitter rivals; founded by an Order defector.
- [[The Sage]]: has spied on the Order for decades. He believes it is a benevolent organization saving the world.
