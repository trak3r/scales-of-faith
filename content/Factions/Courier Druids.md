*A circle of elven and half-elven druids who deliver the mail by animal.*

## History

Behind the scenes, the [[Celtic Courier]] in [[Stonebridge]] is run by a circle of elf and half-elf druids. [[Shelt'n]] is the face behind the counter.

## Goals

- Show that man and nature can coexist, by using **willing animal companions** to deliver messages and packages.

## Presence and Symbols

- Feral looks and earth-tone robes make their druidic background obvious.
- They have no tolerance for disrespect toward service animals.

## Members

- [[Shelt'n]]: proprietor of the Celtic Courier.

## Secrets

> [!secret]- GM Only
> - The druids know about [[Hyde]]'s lycanthropy.
> - If the party is on good terms with them, they lend a small wolf to guide the party to the dire bear in [[Hunt for the Dire Bear]].

## Relationships

- [[Centaur Clan]]: on good terms.
- [[Hunt for the Dire Bear]]: something is hunting their delivery animals on one route.
