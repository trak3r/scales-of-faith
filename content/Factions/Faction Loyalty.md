*To learn an order's secrets, you have to earn the tattoo.*

## The Tattoo

- A large, intricate **dragon** covering the left shoulder and reaching onto the chest and neck.
- The tattoo is also an **enchantment of loyalty** to the church:
  - Staying loyal grants a small bonus to rolls.
  - Defying or betraying the church curses the wearer with a penalty to rolls.

## Earning It

- To learn the secrets of the [[Calla Cabal]] or the [[Order of the Orchid]], a character must earn the tattoo.
- Earning it requires **beating the opposing order to a dragon**.
- By the time of [[Frozen in Ice]], the party should have picked a side.

## Consequences

- Zealots of the opposing order **attack on sight** of the tattoo.
- One particularly psychotic member of the opposing order **collects these tattoos like scalps**.
- The [[Dragon Claw Daggers]] and similar prizes make their owners targets of every faction.
