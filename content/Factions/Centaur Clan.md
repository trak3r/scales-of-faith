*Neutral neighbors, as long as they're left undisturbed.*

## History

The clan has long occupied [[Centaur Valley]], near [[Stonebridge]].

## Goals

- To be left alone.
- They appreciate the natural border that [[The Bulette Grounds]] give them, but will likely rescue innocent victims who wander into trouble there.

## Presence and Symbols

- Townsfolk keep their distance.
- On very rare occasions a centaur wanders into town to shop at [[Brightforge Smithy]].
- Town hunting sometimes causes friction with the clan.

## Members

Use any centaur from your system's bestiary.

## Secrets

> [!secret]- GM Only
> If the party needs rescuing and the GM is feeling charitable, the centaurs make a fine last-minute rescue, especially near the bulette grounds ([[Gordon's Dragon Experience]]).

## Relationships

- [[Hattie's Hats]]: trade agreement (horsehair for buckram).
- [[Courier Druids]]: on good terms.
- [[Hyde]] of [[Hyde's Leatherworks]]: they despise him.

## Hooks

- **A missing youth.** One of the clan's young hasn't returned from a rite-of-passage quest. Tradition forbids the clan to interfere, so they ask the party to keep an eye out and report anything they find. This can overlap with [[Hunt for the Dire Bear]], [[Elven Assassin]], [[Dam the Dire Beavers]], [[Gordon's Dragon Experience]] or any other outdoor adventure.
