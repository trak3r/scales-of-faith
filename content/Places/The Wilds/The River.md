*The river that made Stonebridge, and still feeds it.*

## Description

- Caravans first camped here to water their horses. The shallow crossing became a ferry, then a wooden bridge, then [[The Stone Bridge]].
- Fishing in the river is one of the town's main food sources. The [[Gill Herring|fishermen's guild]] works it hard.
- Upriver lies [[Naiad Falls]]. Downstream, fallen trees sometimes dam the flow.
- The river connects, eventually, to the sea. That's how a shark-man named [[Fintooth]] swam up it and into the town's sewer outlet.

## Hooks

- [[Dam the Dire Beavers]]: the river is rising.
- [[Naiad of the Waterfall]]: a poor spawning season pushes fishermen too far upriver.
