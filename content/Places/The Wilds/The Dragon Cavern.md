*A cavern off a game trail, where something has stood silent for an age.*

> [!map] Map placeholder: The Dragon Cavern
> Battle map, Dungeondraft. Cavern system ending in the statue chamber. See `maps/dragon-cavern/`.

## Description

- Reached by leaving the main road for a game trail through wild-elf country.
- The cavern holds optional filler: a bugbear, a roper, a cave-in, a carrion crawler, or a patrol of orcs and goblins.

## Notable Features

- **The statue.** A massive statue of exquisite detail. It's a little weather-worn and covered in lichen, but otherwise in remarkable shape.

## Secrets

> [!secret]- GM Only
> - The statue is a [[Stone Dragon]].
> - An ancient scroll carried by [[The Black Knight]] tells of the dragon that lived here.
> - The knight smashes it with a *Holy Word of Power* and his maul during [[The First Last Dragon]].
