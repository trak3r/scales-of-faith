*A clearing in the woods where, says one halfling, an invisible dragon lives.*

> [!map] Map placeholder: The Bulette Grounds
> Battle map, Dungeondraft. Clearing with tall grass and loose sand; optional burrow tunnels. See `maps/bulette-grounds/`.

## Description

- A clearing in the woods near [[Centaur Valley]].
- The tall grass is trampled flat.
- There are large footprints in loose sand, too blurred to identify.

## Notable Features

- A tracker can confirm that a **large creature** has been here recently.
- Now and then a **breeze** blows through and the **ground rumbles**.
- (Optional) Burrows beneath the clearing can be explored as a cave system.

## Inhabitants

> [!secret]- GM Only
> A [[Bulette]] that has decided to stop tolerating trespassers. [[Gordon the Dragonologist]] is convinced it's an invisible dragon.

## Hooks

- [[Gordon's Dragon Experience]].
- The [[Centaur Clan]] appreciate the natural border and may rescue innocents in trouble here.
