## Description

### Outside

One of the master's four wives, released by an earlier adventuring party, wanders the grounds. See [[Undead of the Crypt]].

### Entrance

- A stone stairway descends into the tomb. The smell of dust and mildew wafts up.
- Time and weather have worn the steps smooth and treacherous.
- The name above the entrance has been **violently chipped away with a chisel**.
- One of the double doors stands open: somebody has already been here.

### Room 1: The Antechamber

- A dry fountain stands at the center of this circular room. Worthless ancient copper coins lie in it.
- Small semicircular alcoves line the walls, with waist-high shelves for incense and offerings.
- One shelf holds small rodent bones. It's unclear whether they were an offering or a scavenger's snack.

### The Gate

- A thick, rusted gate blocks the way. It can't be broken without extreme force.
- The lock can be picked.
- It creaks **extremely loudly** when opened.

### Room 2: The Four Wives

- Four crude stone coffins stand evenly around this circular room. The one to the southwest is already open (see Outside).
- They hold the remains of the master's four wives. Each emits a horrible stench when opened. Characters caught in the cloud must pass a constitution check or vomit uncontrollably.
- Each wife wears moderately valuable jewelry.
- The **fourth wife**, in the northeast corner, was still alive when the master died and when she was entombed. She is now a zombie, and she wears [[The Heart of Desire]].

### The Stone Door

- The way onward is blocked by a large circular stone set into the walls. There are no hinges and no scrape marks to hint at how it works.
- There is no way to open it, because it was never meant to be opened. Rolling it aside takes immense strength or clever ingenuity.

### Room 3: The Master

- The master's stone coffin rests on a raised section at the center of the room.
- If the coffin is opened recklessly, **the master rises and attacks**.
- Beneath his wrappings he wears a wealth of jewelry. The bottom of his coffin is lined with exotic coins, some of them large rectangular plates.
- **Statues** (optional): one or more of the statues is a stone golem that activates when anyone approaches the coffin.
- **The Artifact** sits in the northern alcove: [[The Ebony Dragon]].

### Secret Passage

The original workmen built a back door so they could rob the crypt later. For reasons unknown, they never did.

## Secrets

> [!secret]- GM Only
> - [[The Ebony Dragon]] raises anything that dies in Room 3 as undead.
> - The name chiseled off the entrance, and the dragon artifact inside, make this tomb a candidate for [[The Savior]]'s unknown resting place. That's an open question, not established lore.
