## Description

The cave entrance is littered with bones, very much like the killer rabbit's lair in *Monty Python and the Holy Grail*.

## Notable Features

- During the **day**, the bear is probably asleep inside.
- At **night**, it's probably out in the woods.
- Among the litter of corpses: plenty of coin, some gems, perhaps a valuable item, and a *lot* of undelivered messages and packages from the [[Celtic Courier]].
