## Description

A waterfall upriver from [[Stonebridge]] spills into a **very deep pool**. A **pact of peace** between the town and the naiads keeps fishermen away, a truce brokered by [[Truman the Druid]].

## Notable Features

- A **cave behind the waterfall**.
- A **cache of gems** scattered across the bottom of the pool.
- Dangerous fish.
- A cool magic item for the GM to choose.

## Inhabitants

- A family of [[Naiad|naiads]].
- A water weird, and possibly frogmen.

## Hooks

- [[Naiad of the Waterfall]]: fishermen are breaking the pact.
