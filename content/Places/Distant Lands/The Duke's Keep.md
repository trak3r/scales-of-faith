*A duke claims to keep a captive dragon in the amphitheater beneath his keep.*

## Description

- Beneath the keep, the duke has built an **enormous amphitheater** with no seats.
- The main stage holds "the dragon" behind enormous iron bars that run from floor to ceiling.
- Giant **flaming braziers** keep the room unpleasantly hot, to sell the illusion of a dragon's lair.

## Notable Features

- **The visitation balcony** is far enough from the stage to protect the deception.
- Visitors are **disarmed** before meeting the dragon.
- The balcony is an **anti-magic zone**.
- The entryway onto the balcony is a **dispel magic portal** that disenchants any magic item carried through it. Visitors are warned! If they sneak something in and it gets destroyed, they made their choice.

## Secrets

> [!secret]- GM Only
> - **The dragon is a puppet**: an elaborate mechanism worked by a team of gnomes, with a dash of illusion magic.
> - Its booming voice comes from an enchanted megaphone.
> - The duke uses the "dragon" as a false oracle to deceive and coerce allies and enemies.
