*A statue in a lake of lava atop an active volcano.*

## Description

- Atop an **active volcano**, a stone dragon sits in a **lake of lava**.
- Earlier statues have been worn and weathered stone, but this one is rumored to be **obsidian, in exquisite detail**.

## Notable Features

- **Failed crossings** litter the shore: iron boats, long ladders, catapults, ballistae on chains.

## Secrets

> [!secret]- GM Only
> - The statue is a [[Stone Dragon]].
> - The [[Order of the Orchid]] has conscripted gnomes to invent an elaborate contraption for crossing the lava. The wreckage on the shore is from their earlier attempts.
