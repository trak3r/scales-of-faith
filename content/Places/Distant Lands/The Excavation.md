*Diggers have unearthed the head of a buried stone dragon, and they want it gone.*

## Description

- An excavation has just uncovered the **head of a buried stone dragon**.
- The diggers see it as a **bad omen** and want the party to get rid of it.

## Secrets

> [!secret]- GM Only
> **It's a fake**, a man-carved statue:
> - Crude, with little detail. The head is far more detailed than the body.
> - Its proportions are all wrong: eyes too big, snout too long.
> - The [[Order of the Orchid]] has no interest in it.

## Hooks

- Hired to "eradicate" a bad omen.
- A test of the party's growing expertise. Can they tell a fake from the real thing?
