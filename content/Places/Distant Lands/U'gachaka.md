*A faraway jungle where a tribe worships the statue of a winged snake.*

## Description

- A jungle so far away that reaching it takes **weeks**, by ship around the horn or overland.
- A **tribe** with a unique language lives here. They are courteous hosts, tolerant up to a point.

## Notable Features

- **The sacred statue** of a winged snake. It's so covered in ornamental wreaths and offerings that its legs are hidden, which is why it's mistaken for a snake.
- The tribe **will not tolerate shenanigans** around it.
- **Jungle perils:** giant leeches, killer vines, carnivorous plants and swarms of tiny thieving monkeys. See [[Jungle Perils]].
- A **healing natural spring** where travelers can fill their waterskins.
- The **remains of another party**, with one random valuable item.

## Inhabitants

- The tribe (an interpreter is required).
- A hostile neighboring tribe with poison-tipped weapons, possibly cannibals.

## Secrets

> [!secret]- GM Only
> - The winged snake is a [[Stone Dragon]].
> - The tribe and statue still exist, even though the only account of them is very old.
> - If the [[Order of the Orchid]] arrives and tries to destroy the statue, the tribe blames the party and attacks, unless the party helps defend it.
> - If the [[Calla Cabal]] arrives and makes the statue disappear, the tribe blames the party for bringing them and attacks.
