## Description

The main hangout for the player characters.

## Notable Features

- **A fully stocked bar**, thanks to [[The Holy Spirits]].
- **Delicious meats**, courtesy of [[Ground and Pound]].
- **A private back room** for special guests, garishly decorated with a hearth, sconces and mounted trophies: deer, boar and one exotic lion. See [[Dinner with the Sage]].

## Inhabitants

- [[Seraphina]], proprietor.
- [[Brock Blusterhaus]] can almost always be found here, drunk and telling tall tales.

## Hooks

- The opening session's robbery happens here: [[In Media Res]].
- As needed, Seraphina steers the party toward other events in the campaign.
- A serving girl who overhears talk of dragons may trade a tip about Brock's "winged snake" story for a reward ([[Dinner with the Sage]]).
