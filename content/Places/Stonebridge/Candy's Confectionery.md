*Lozenges and lollipops with a little alchemical kick.*

## Description

A sweet shop whose candies carry the same effects as potions, just a little less potent.

## Notable Features

| Candy | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Black licorice | See in the dark |
| Cinnamon | Fire breath |
| Mint | Frost breath |
| Saltwater taffy | Water breathing |
| Valerian | Sleepiness |

## Inhabitants

- [[Candy Milton]], proprietor.

## Hooks

- **Honey run.** Collect honey from giant bees while avoiding a pesky druid (perhaps [[Truman the Druid]]).
