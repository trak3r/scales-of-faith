*Gems and jewelry, appraised by a goblin in a tailored suit.*

## Description

The shop smells heavily of cologne.

## Notable Features

- Appraises, buys and sells gems and jewelry.
- Can set gems into amulets, such as the [[Sapphire of the Sea]].

## Inhabitants

- [[Jerem]], proprietor.

## Secrets

> [!secret]- GM Only
> - Jerem's loupe can **detect magic**, and he'll try to low-ball powerful items.
> - He's probably colluding with the fence at [[Nick's Knacks]].

## Hooks

- **The dice set.** Jerem has an incomplete set of jewels shaped like gaming dice. He'll pay handsomely for the missing pieces, such as a diamond d10 and a black opal d20.
