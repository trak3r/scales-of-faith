*A candle shop whose name is a joke, and whose back stock isn't.*

## Description

- Locals mispronounce the priest's name, O'Connell, as "carnal," so he named the shop as a joke.
- The **former owner was an occultist**.

## Notable Features

- **Gold and white candles**: expensive, and they grant a one-day *blessing* if burned the night before.
- **Black and red candles**: sinister leftovers from the previous owner, which can have adverse effects.

## Inhabitants

- [[Father O'Connell]], proprietor.

## Hooks

- **Exorcise the candles.** Take part in a ritual to cleanse the evil candles, which summons minor demons to defeat.
- **Mammoth tallow.** Hunt a woolly mammoth for its massive supply of tallow.
