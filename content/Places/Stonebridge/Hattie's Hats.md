*A millinery whose roof looks suspiciously like a witch's hat.*

## Description

A shop making women's hats. Its roof somewhat resembles a witch's hat, which doesn't help the rumors about its owner.

## Notable Features

- Witch and wizard hats.
- Fancy, fashionable hats with certain feathers that can **enhance charisma** or charm attempts.
- **The disguise hat.** One particular hat subtly disguises its wearer's face, changing the nose, eyebrows and cheekbones just enough to escape recognition.

## Inhabitants

- [[Hattie Bösch]], proprietor.

## Hooks

- **Horsehair trade.** Hattie trades with the [[Centaur Clan]] for horsehair to make buckram. The party could carry the goods.
