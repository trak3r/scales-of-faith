*A sweltering forge whose dwarf smith is better than she lets on.*

## Description

- It smells of burning wood, coal and molten iron.
- When she's working, the clanging of hammer on anvil is ear-piercing.
- The furnace heat is sweltering. Heavily clothed characters can't stay long before sweating profusely.

## Notable Features

- Sale and repair of armor, weapons, horseshoes and the like.
- **The bargain barrels.** *"[[Olen]]'s mercs buy all my good stuff. If you want something now, you'll have to dig through the barrels."* Two barrels in the corner hold a few dozen old, rusted, well-used weapons. A character must make a luck roll to find one without a −1 penalty to attack or damage. She sells them super cheap just to be rid of them.

## Inhabitants

- [[Bridgette Brightforge]], proprietor.
- On very rare occasions, a centaur from the [[Centaur Clan]] comes in to shop.

## Secrets

> [!secret]- GM Only
> Bridgette could craft masterwork gear but deliberately doesn't. See [[Bridgette Brightforge]].

## Hooks

- **The posse.** A band of heavily armed dwarves rolls into town looking for someone who fits her description. The party must decide whom to help, or stay out of it and watch.
