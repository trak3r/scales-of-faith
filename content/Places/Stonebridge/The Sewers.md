## Description

### Water

- Waist-high stagnant water, too dirty to see through.
- It's viscous enough to **slow movement**.
- Oil slicks on the surface are a **fire hazard**.

### Gas

- It smells of exactly what it is.
- Characters make repeated constitution checks. Poisonous gas causes nausea, vomiting and light-headedness.
- Pockets of methane are a **fire hazard**.

### Creepy Crawlies

Rats, snakes, centipedes and spiders. The occasional odd-looking fish. The bones of small animals.

## Notable Features

- **Grates and gates.** Rusty bars block certain paths. Even unlocked grates are rusted shut and must be forced open. Opening one makes a loud squeak that echoes through the tunnels and should trigger an encounter check.
- **Pit traps.** Here and there a deep hole drops away beneath the murky water. Encumbered characters sink to the bottom. A character probing ahead with a pole will notice them.
- **Guard patrols and posts.** Some corners, intersections and halls are watched by [[Fintooth's Minions]].
- **Entrances everywhere.** There are so many sewer entrances that the minions can reach the entire town. The main entrance is under [[The Stone Bridge]].
- **Hyde's cell.** On full moons, [[Hyde]] locks himself in a cell down here ([[Full Moon]]).

## Inhabitants

- [[Fintooth]] and [[Fintooth's Minions]], from his driftwood throne.
- Assorted horrors: see [[Creatures of the Sewers]].

## Secrets

> [!secret]- GM Only
> - The slime lurks under a "lucky" grate that townsfolk treat like a wishing well. It has collected a pile of cheap coins.
> - A small brass key in the giant rat's nest can open whatever the GM likes.
