## Description

### History

- **Riverside.** The town began as a riverside camp for caravans, a good place to stop for the night and water the horses. Some camps grew permanent and became a trading post where passing caravans could restock and get a hot meal. Tents became shacks, and shacks became buildings.
- **Ferryford.** The shallow crossing spawned a ferry for the seasons of deeper water and stronger currents. A restaurant added rooms and became an inn. An inn needs a stable, and a stable needs a blacksmith for shoeing and a leatherworker for saddles. The ferry was eventually replaced by a sturdy wooden bridge.
- **Stonebridge.** The cluster of homes and businesses needed governing, so the first mayor was elected. The first order of business was to reinforce the wooden bridge and eventually replace it with stone. The townsfolk debated and voted on an official town name, but everyone kept calling it Stonebridge, so they gave up.

### Remoteness

- The town is so far from any capital that all news is ancient history. By the time word arrives of a new king's coronation, he has already died of old age or been overthrown.
- Fresh faces from far lands always carry completely unrecognizable coinage.
- The town has no strategic value for borders or warfare, so armies never pass through.

### Commerce

- Being so remote, the town imports and exports no perishable goods (meat, produce, eggs). This makes **livestock extremely valuable**.
- The main food sources are fishing in [[The River]] and hunting. Hunting sometimes causes trouble with the [[Centaur Clan]] and [[Truman the Druid]].
- No nobility lives here, so luxury goods are hard to sell. There are a few wealthy residents, such as [[Olen]] of [[The Holy Spirits]], but they have no use for precious baubles.

## Notable Features

- **[[The Stone Bridge]]**: the town's namesake, with a troll living underneath.
- **[[The Sewers]]**: a maze beneath every street, with entrances all over town.
- **The jail**, across from the [[Mayor's Office]], one of the oldest buildings in town.
