*Stonebridge's butcher and slaughterhouse. Breathe through your mouth.*

## Description

- The stench is so rank that characters must make a constitution check to avoid retching.
- The flies are so thick that several are always on your face. Their buzzing is so loud you have to nearly shout to hold a conversation.

## Notable Features

- Sells meat, and supplies [[The Slattern Tavern]].
- Buys **fresh** meat from slain **exotic** creatures. Dire bear meat fetches about 2 gp ([[Hunt for the Dire Bear]]).
- [[Hyde]] buys a slab of meat here every full moon ([[Full Moon]]).

## Inhabitants

- [[Grella]], proprietor.
