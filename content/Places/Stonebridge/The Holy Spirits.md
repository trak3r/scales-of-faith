*A liquor shop dressed up as a church, the front door of a massive brewing estate.*

## Description

- The shop's facade and interior look like a church, and many locals are not amused.
- The shop is one small corner of a **massive estate** with a brewery and a vineyard out back.
- When the wind blows the right way, you can smell the brewery from a mile off.

## Notable Features

- Sells every liquor known to man.
- The business is enormously lucrative, and plenty of well-armed guards patrol the property.
- Supplies the bar at [[The Slattern Tavern]].

## Inhabitants

- [[Olen]], proprietor, master brewer and financial genius.

## Hooks

- **Test the security.** Olen hires the party to test his estate security, without killing anyone or causing damage. They must acquire a particular bottle of wine and return it to him.
