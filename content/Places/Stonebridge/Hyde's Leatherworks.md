*Fine leather goods and a buyer for exotic hides.*

## Description

The town's leatherworker and tanner. When Stonebridge was still a ferry crossing, its stable needed a leatherworker for saddles, and the trade has been here ever since.

## Notable Features

- Sells fine leather armor, packs, tents and saddles.
- Buys exotic hides.

## Inhabitants

- [[Hyde]], proprietor.

## Secrets

> [!secret]- GM Only
> - Hyde is a **were-rat**. See [[Hyde]] and [[Full Moon]].
> - A locked, trapped chest behind his bed holds a suit of leather armor made from a displacer beast's hide, which gives the wearer the same benefits in combat.

## Hooks

- **The dire bear pelt.** Hyde needs a dire bear pelt to make a cloak for a local noble, and it may be the same beast that's harassing the [[Celtic Courier]]. See [[Hunt for the Dire Bear]].

## Relationships

- The [[Centaur Clan]] despises him.
- [[Shamrock Loams]] has noticed his suspicious behavior.
