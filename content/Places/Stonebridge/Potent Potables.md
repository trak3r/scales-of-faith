*Potions, alchemy, and custom blends from an ancient ranger.*

## Description

The town's potion shop and arcanist. Its owner's lifelong obsession with herbs and brews eventually led him to alchemy.

## Notable Features

- **Custom potions** that combine effects, such as *remove poison* with *cure minor wounds*.
- **Cutting and diluting**: one powerful potion can be split into several smaller doses with weaker effects.
- The shop is the glassblower's best customer.

## Inhabitants

- [[Gilf]], proprietor.

## Hooks

- **Fragile cargo.** Deliver a wagon full of fragile potion bottles, in sealed crates packed with straw, along a long, bumpy road.
