*Miscellany, artifacts and collectibles. Ask about the back room.*

## Description

A pawn shop full of miscellany, artifacts and collectibles. The front of the shop holds genuinely valuable items and works as a legitimate pawnbroker.

## Notable Features

- Nick refers mundane goods to the appropriate shop: [[Brightforge Smithy]], [[Jerem's Jewelry]] and so on.

## Inhabitants

- [[Nick]], proprietor.

## Secrets

> [!secret]- GM Only
> - Nick is a **fence**. A back-room entrance leads to a secret basement full of contraband, and that's where the good stuff is.
> - He's probably colluding with [[Jerem]].

## Hooks

- **A buyer for the unbuyable.** Nick needs a buyer for an exotic, powerful item in his back room.
