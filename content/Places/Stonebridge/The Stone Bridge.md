*The town's namesake: a stone span over the river with a troll underneath.*

## Description

The first mayor's first order of business was to replace the wooden bridge with stone. It still stands, and it gave the town its name ([[Stonebridge]]).

## Notable Features

- The **main entrance to [[The Sewers]]** opens beneath the bridge.

## Inhabitants

- [[Turd the Troll]] lives underneath. He "guards" the sewer entrance, but doesn't actually stop anyone coming or going, as long as they leave him alone or bring a treat.

## Secrets

> [!secret]- GM Only
> - The fishermen's guild, at [[Gill Herring]]'s insistence, leaves Turd a barrel of fish heads.
> - Some of [[Fintooth's Minions]] worship Turd, and he is an unwitting ally of [[Fintooth]].
