*A coastal town climbing from the docks to the hilltop, and the gateway to Dragon Reef.*

> [!map] Map placeholder: Shoreside
> Region/town map, Wonderdraft or Dungeondraft. Beach, docks, warehouses, and the hilltop road. See `maps/shoreside-town/`.

## Description

- Built on an incline, from the beach up to a road along the crest of the hill.
- **The closer to the water, the poorer.** The working lower class lives by the docks.
- **The higher up, the wealthier.** The upper class, who *own* the dockside businesses, live near the top.
- The road between Shoreside and [[Stonebridge]] is patrolled by guards from time to time ([[Guard Patrol]]).

## Notable Features

- [[Dragon Reef]] lies offshore. It's a tourist attraction and a good-luck charm for sailors, fishermen and pearl divers.
- **Lodging:** [[The Prawn Bucket]] (cheap) and the [[Seaview Inn]] (less cheap).
- A large dockside **warehouse**, where a raised statue would be stored ([[Raising the Reef]]).

## Inhabitants

- Dockworkers, fishermen, pearl divers and the merchants who own them.
- A **barge captain** who refuses to sail unless the seas are calm.
- A suspicious **captain** looking to hire independent mercenaries ([[Raising the Reef]]).

## Secrets

> [!secret]- GM Only
> - A mysterious organization with "deep pockets" (the [[Calla Cabal]]) has made a "sizable donation" to the town for the rights to "raise the reef." Townsfolk who profit from the reef are not happy.
> - Cabal agents are lodging at the [[Seaview Inn]].

## Hooks

- [[Raising the Reef]].
- [[Sea Voyage]]: ships for hire, and a captain with a tale of a dragon-shaped cliff.
