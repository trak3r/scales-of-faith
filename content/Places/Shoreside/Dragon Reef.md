## Description

- A reef in the shape of a **flying dragon**, covered in coral, with oysters clinging to it.
- It's shrouded in a **forest of seaweed**.

## Notable Features

- A **tourist attraction** for visitors to [[Shoreside]].
- A **good-luck token** for sailors, fishermen and pearl divers.

## Inhabitants

- Sahuagin raiders, a chuul and a giant oyster. See [[Creatures of the Reef]].

## Secrets

> [!secret]- GM Only
> - The reef formed around an underwater [[Stone Dragon]], posed **in flight**.
> - [[Fintooth]] visited it in his open-sea days. He'll trade a map to "the untold treasures of Dragon Reef" for his life.

## Hooks

- **Leads:** [[The Sage]]; [[Fintooth]] from [[Fishy Business]]; or the [[Calla Cabal]] or [[Order of the Orchid]], if the party has pledged loyalty.
