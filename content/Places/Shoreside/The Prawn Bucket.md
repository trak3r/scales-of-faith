*The cheapest "inn" closest to the docks. Don't leave anything unattended.*

## Description

- Clearly converted from an old stable.
- The "rooms" are former stalls, retrofitted with thin makeshift walls and doors.
- The southern wall faces an alley where drunkards relieve themselves, and the smell fills the inn.

## Notable Features

Each room has:

- one small straw-stuffed bed, surely infested with some itching insect;
- one chair, which breaks under heavy characters;
- one small table, which wobbles and doesn't sit level;
- one wooden bucket. Don't ask what it's for.

The Prawn Bucket has **complete vacancy** and can be haggled down cheap for the whole party.

## Secrets

> [!secret]- GM Only
> Any valuables left unattended here **will** disappear.
