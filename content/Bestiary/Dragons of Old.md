*What the dragons were before the Stoning, and what they'll be when they wake.*

## Description

> [!secret]- GM Only
> - The dragons fought mankind for centuries in the [[The War of the Wyrm|War of the Wyrm]].
> - Some had legs and wings that could be mistaken for a winged snake once the legs were hidden ([[U'gachaka]]).
> - A revived dragon believes no time has passed since the Stoning, and is understandably confused and furious ([[Fate of the Dragons]]).
> - In [[The Plane of Dreams]], they are blurry silhouettes against a neon sky, with deep, rumbling voices and the smell of lavender and sulfur ([[Dragon Eye Orb]]).
