*A statue of exquisite detail, frozen mid-gesture in an unlikely place.*

## Description

- A massive statue of a dragon, extraordinarily lifelike, posed exactly as the creature was at one instant: sleeping, crouching, swimming, in flight.
- Usually weathered by age and overgrown with lichen, moss, vines, coral or seaweed, sometimes so badly that it's mistaken for a boulder or a reef.
- At least one is rumored to be obsidian rather than weathered stone ([[Lake of Fire]]).

## Behavior and Tactics

> [!secret]- GM Only
> - It *is* a dragon, petrified by [[The Stoning]]. It doesn't act at all, unless revived.
> - **Destroying one:** the [[Order of the Orchid]] uses a *Holy Word of Power* and a heavy two-handed weapon.
> - **Reviving one:** the [[Calla Cabal]] can revive one. The dragon wakes believing no time has passed ([[Fate of the Dragons]]).

## Stat Suggestion

As an object: very hard, very heavy. Lifting it takes a barge and levitation magic ([[Raising the Reef]]). Once revived, use [[Dragons of Old]].
