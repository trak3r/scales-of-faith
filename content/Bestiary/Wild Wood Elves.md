*Treetop archers who ambush travelers on the game trails.*

## Description

A band of wild wood elves who claim the deep forest off the main roads.

## Behavior and Tactics

- They ambush parties that leave the main road for a game trail.
- Most of them post up **in trees** and attack with short bows.
- They may set a couple of **trained wolves** on the party from the ground.

## Stat Suggestion

Use any elf scouts or bandit archers, plus wolves.
