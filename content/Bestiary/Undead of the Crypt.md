*A master, his four wives, and the statues that guard them.*

## The Wandering Wife

- Released by an earlier adventuring party, she now wanders the grounds outside.
- **Stat suggestion:** coffer corpse, or any relentless corpse that keeps rising.

## The Fourth Wife

- She was still alive when the master died, and when she was entombed. Now she is a zombie.
- She wears [[The Heart of Desire]].
- **Stat suggestion:** zombie.

## The Master

- He rises and attacks if his coffin is opened recklessly.
- He is adorned with a myriad of valuable jewelry, and lies on a bed of exotic coins.
- **Stat suggestion:** mummy.

## The Statues (optional)

- One or more statues in the master's chamber are a stone golem, which activates when anyone approaches the coffin.
- **Stat suggestion:** stone golem.

## The Ebony Dragon's Curse

Anything that dies in the master's chamber rises as undead ([[The Ebony Dragon]]).
