*Ordinary animals grown huge and wrong.*

## Description

*"Dire creatures often have a demonic or aberrant appearance, with sharp fangs, bony ridges, or glowing eyes. This reflects their connection to otherworldly forces or their twisted nature."*

## Dire Bear

- **Behavior:** usually asleep in its cave by day. It won't leave in daylight unless it must, and waits for attackers to come to it. By night it hunts in the woods, or waits for intruders to enter the cave and then blocks the entrance.
- **Stat suggestion:** dire bear, or a large cave bear.
- **Treasure:** its hide (around 6 gp from [[Hyde]] if intact) and its meat (around 2 gp from [[Ground and Pound]]).
- **Found in:** [[Hunt for the Dire Bear]], [[Dire Bear Cave]].

## Dire Beavers

- **Behavior:** they attack by surprise while the party is investigating or clearing their dam.
- **Stat suggestion:** giant beaver or prehistoric beaver.
- **Found in:** [[Dam the Dire Beavers]].
