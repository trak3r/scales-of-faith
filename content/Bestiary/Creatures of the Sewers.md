*Everything else living in the filth beneath Stonebridge.*

## Slime

- Invisible beneath the surface of the water.
- **Stat suggestion,** by difficulty:
  - *Beginner:* green slime (low HP, acid damage).
  - *Manageable:* gray ooze (destroys equipment).
  - *Hard-hitting:* ochre jelly (lots of HP).
  - *Boss:* black pudding (destroys equipment).
  - Or yellow mold.
- **Treasure:** a pile of cheap coins, collected while it lurked under a "lucky" grate that townsfolk treat like a wishing well.

## Carrion Crawler

- Found munching on an unidentifiable corpse at a dead end.
- **Stat suggestion:** carrion crawler, or any paralyzing tentacled scavenger.
- **Treasure:** gut it for jewelry, gems and coins. Rusted, worthless weapons and armor lie scattered around its lair. Buried deep is a wax-sealed *scroll of light*.

## Giant Rat

- Leaps out of a shoulder-high hole in a hallway wall.
- **Stat suggestion:** giant rat.
- **Treasure:** at the back of the hole, for anyone who dares stick an arm in, is a collection of trinkets, including a small brass key (for the GM to use as they like).

## Giant Spider

- Drops from a vertical shaft in the ceiling of an intersection.
- **Stat suggestion:** giant spider.
- **Treasure:** in the dense webs above are the belongings of past victims, including a *potion of barkskin*, which may fall and break if the webs are disturbed roughly.

## Skeletons

- The remains of past adventurers, still armed and armored, trapped in a gated room.
- **Stat suggestion:** skeleton.
- **Treasure:** old, cheap, worthless gear, plus some coins and gems. One wears *bracers of archery*.
