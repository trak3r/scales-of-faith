*What lives around Dragon Reef, and what doesn't want it raised.*

## Sahuagin

- Fish people who try to board the barge, perhaps even attacking during the voyage.
- They have **shark telepathy and can ride sharks**!
- **Stat suggestion:** sahuagin.
- **Treasure:** miscellaneous baubles (rings, gems, coins). One wears a *belt of endurance*.

## Chuul

- A lobster-like tentacled horror.
- **Stat suggestion:** chuul, or any giant armored crustacean.
- **Treasure:** if gutted, 200 gp worth of jewelry, gems and coins, including a *ring of water walking*.

## Giant Oyster

- Tries to grab characters and drown them.
- **Stat suggestion:** giant clam.
- **Treasure:** valuable mother-of-pearl inside the shell, and a large yellow pearl worth 200 gp.
