*Gordon's "invisible dragon."*

## Description

A burrowing predator whose passage feels like a breeze and sounds like the ground rumbling.

## Behavior and Tactics

- It has decided to **stop tolerating trespassers** in its clearing.
- It bursts up from below after a telltale rumble.
- Its burrows can be explored as a cave system.

## Stat Suggestion

Use any bulette.

## Treasure

- [[The Barb]] is stuck in its flank (or substitute a weapon reward that suits the party).
