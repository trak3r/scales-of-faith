*A black-armored paladin of the Order of the Orchid.*

## Description

Technically, they are paladins.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Armor** | Black-enameled plate. Never a shield, since they only use two-handed weapons. |
| **Weapon** | One of: claymore, warhammer (the knight in [[The First Last Dragon]] wields a maul). Always two-handed. |
| **Inventory** | The Holy Book of the Order; historical records (scrolls) of nearby dragon lairs. |
| **Skills** | *Holy Word of Power* (for smashing stone dragons); general military training (tactics, survival, first aid); *bless*; *cure minor wounds*; *light*; *shield*; *Guarded Slumber*; *push*. |
| **Resistances** | Charm, sleep, feeblemind, confusion. |

## Behavior and Tactics

- Militant, disciplined and loyal. Not easily fooled or riled to anger.
- Includes *Guarded Slumber* in every night's prayers, so a knight's camp is never surprised. See [[Spells]].
- Keeps the holy book and lair records packed close, even using the knapsack as a pillow ([[The First Last Dragon]]).

## Stat Suggestion

Use any paladin or veteran knight from your system, with the resistances above.
