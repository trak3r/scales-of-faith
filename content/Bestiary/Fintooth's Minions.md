*Sneaky little fishfolk with tridents and nets, raiding Stonebridge from below.*

## Description

Small, sneaky, impressionable and relatively weak.

## Behavior and Tactics

- Armed with **tridents and nets**, for grappling and crowd control.
- They raid the town for barrels of fish, using the many sewer entrances.
- They patrol and guard corners, intersections and halls of [[The Sewers]].
- Some of them worship [[Turd the Troll]].

## Stat Suggestion

Any of these, scaled down:

- **Sahuagin** (with shark telepathy). You may need a less threatening "pygmy" version.
- **Troglodytes**.
- **Kobolds**.

## Treasure

They pay their tribute to [[Fintooth]], so the treasure is in his hoard.

## Found In

- [[Fishy Business]], [[The Sewers]]
