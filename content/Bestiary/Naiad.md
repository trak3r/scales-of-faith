*Water spirits of the falls upriver, bound to the town by an old pact.*

## Description

A family of naiads living in the pool at [[Naiad Falls]].

## Behavior and Tactics

- **Charm.**
- **Healing.**
- **Water breathing.**
- **Blinding fog.**
- Resistant to cold, magic, and slashing and piercing weapons.
- Superior stealth.

## Stat Suggestion

Use any naiad, nymph or water spirit.
