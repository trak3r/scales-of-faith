*A master list of businesses and institutions for building out any town or castle, transcribed from the author's journal.*

Several of these became the shops of [[Stonebridge]]. The rest are free for the taking.

## Trades and Crafts

- Blacksmith
- Armory, armorer, weapons
- Bowyer, arrows, bowstrings, targets
- Skinner, tanner, smelter
- Clothier, tailor
- Cobbler, shoes
- Hat maker
- Weaving, baskets
- Hemp, rope maker
- Oil, fat renderer, soap, kerosene, tar
- Candle shop
- Barrel maker
- Furniture maker, carpenter, chests
- Wagon and carriage maker
- Boat maker
- Stone carver, statues, sculptor
- Pottery, potter, clay, kiln
- Puppet maker, marionettes, toy maker
- Musical instruments
- Scientist, inventor, mechanic
- Construction company, contractors
- Quarry, stone supplier, quartz, marble

## Food and Drink

- Inn
- Taverns: seedy vs. upscale
- Brewmaster
- Vineyard, wine
- Grocer
- Butcher
- Bakery, bread, pastries
- Candy shop
- Dairy, milk, cheesemonger
- Fishery, fishmonger
- Chef, cooking school, culinary
- Market
- General store, mining equipment

## Animals and Farming

- Stable
- Kennel: dogs, cats, birds
- Pigs
- Apiary, honey bees
- Pens for cows, chickens, goats, sheep
- Chicken coop, pigeons, hawk house, falconer
- Animal trainer, husbandry, lion tamer: hunting dogs, guard dogs, messenger birds
- Veterinarian, animal doctor
- Fertilizer
- Aquarium, terrarium: piranhas, eels, snakes

## Magic and the Uncanny

- Alchemist, potions, apothecary, scrolls
- Occultist, weird stuff, wands, staves
- Eccentric wizard
- Fortune teller, voodoo, holistic
- Ghostbuster, exorcist, cleric
- Coroner, necromancer, funeral guy
- Mortician (with the taxidermist)
- Taxidermist
- Botanist, herbs
- Florist, druid? Dryad-ist?
- Tattoo artist, studio, magic tattoos, parlor

## Health

- Doctor, physician, surgeon, medicine, pharmacy
- Dentist
- Optometrist, glasses
- Barber, shave
- Gym, muscle guys, wrestlers

## Law and Government

- Courthouse, gallows
- Jail
- Town hall, square
- Sheriff, bailiff, executioner, magistrate
- Barracks, militia, fort, training
- Gate, guard tower
- Knights' hall, squires
- Foreign ambassador, embassy
- VIP residences: mayor, rich man

## Services

- Lawyer, accountant, bank
- Investigator, detective
- Bounty hunter, mercenaries
- Bulletin board: jobs, quests, bounties, warrants
- Guide: forest, jungle, desert, explorer
- Courier, deliveries, local messenger, urchin
- Post office, owls, arcane communication
- Carriage or wagon for hire
- Import, export, caravans, hiring guards
- Marina, travel and shipping
- Translator, interpreter
- Day care, kids of all races

## Learning and Culture

- Bookstore
- School, traditional and magic; university
- Astronomer, star charts
- Cartographer, maps
- Navigator (with the cartographer)
- Newspaper, reporter, journalist
- Printer: books, pamphlets, posters
- Author, writer, novelist: romance and adventure
- Theater, chorus, circus, bards
- Traveling troupe, circus, freaks
- Dance studio
- Dojo, martial arts, wrestling
- Monastery, monks
- Temples
- Various guild "halls," basements
- Renowned hunter, mounted trophies

## Vice

- Con man, real estate, scams, grifts
- Casino: cards, dice, skooma, bouncers
- Drug den, opium, hookah lounge

## Infrastructure

- Fountain, garden, hedge maze
- Statue(s)
- Well, cistern
- Sewers
- Dump, landfill, garbage pile: attracts giant rats and shambling mounds
