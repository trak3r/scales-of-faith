*Roll for the weather each day and each night.*

Written for the coast at [[Shoreside]] ([[Raising the Reef]]), but it works anywhere.

| d6 | Weather |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | A heavy storm has the entire town battened down. |
| 2 | Overcast, with constant light, dreary rain. |
| 3 | Clear skies, but strong winds make for rough seas. |
| 4 | Pea-soup fog so thick you can't see your hand in front of your face. |
| 5 | Clear and calm: sunny, with a slight breeze. |
| 6 | Blistering heat, no breeze. Sweat and sunburn. |

## At Sea

- The barge captain in [[Raising the Reef]] refuses to sail unless the seas are calm.
- Some coin might change his mind, but he'll be short-handed: half the crew value their lives.
