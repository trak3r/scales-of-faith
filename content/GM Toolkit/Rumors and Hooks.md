*Tavern gossip, shop side quests and people who point the way. Drop them into play whenever the party needs a nudge.*

## Who Points the Way

Two locals can steer the party toward any adventure in this wiki:

- [[Seraphina]] at [[The Slattern Tavern]] steers the characters toward other events in the campaign as needed.
- [[Mayor Midge]] directs the characters to events and adventures as needed, such as [[Naiad of the Waterfall]].

## Tavern Talk

Things the party might overhear at [[The Slattern Tavern]] or around town:

- [[Brock Blusterhaus]] tells drunken tall tales of "his" fantastical adventures, including a **winged snake** in a jungle far away. This leads to [[Journey to U'gachaka]].
- People say there's something unnatural about [[Seraphina]]'s beauty and charisma. Is she half-elven, a vampire, an angel?
- People say [[Hattie Bösch]] is a witch.
- Some say a troll lives under [[The Stone Bridge]]. The Fishermen's Guild leaves him a barrel of fish heads. See [[Turd the Troll]].
- An adventuring party went to explore [[The Crypt]] and never came back. [[Matty Mooseburgher]] waited three days and three nights for them before heading home alone.
- A robed figure with a gnarled staff is sometimes glimpsed in the trees. Folk say it's [[Truman the Druid]].
- Barrels of fish keep disappearing ([[Fishy Business]]).
- The river is rising, and two lumberjacks who went to check it haven't come back ([[Dam the Dire Beavers]]).
- Fishermen are going too far upriver after a poor spawning season ([[Naiad of the Waterfall]]).
- A traveling troupe's fliers announce the coming [[Modern Times Festival]].

## Dragon Rumors

- A dragon-related artifact is *rumored* to be traveling with a particular merchant caravan ([[Wagon Train Heist]]).
- A royal auction is offering a pair of daggers made from real dragon claws ([[Royal Auction]]).
- A duke claims to have imprisoned a dragon ([[Puppetmaster]]).
- A ship captain speaks of a cliffside rock outcrop that looks like a dragon ([[Sea Voyage]]).
- An excavation has just unearthed the head of a buried stone dragon ([[The Excavation]]).
- A dragon frozen in ice has been revealed by a melting glacier ([[Frozen in Ice]]).
- A stone dragon on an island in a lake of lava is said to be obsidian ([[Crossing the Lake of Fire]]).
