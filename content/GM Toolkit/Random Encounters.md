A [[Knight of the Order]] in the party's company casts *Guarded Slumber* every night, so there are no midnight encounters while traveling with one ([[Spells]]).

## Sewers

Opening a rusted grate in [[The Sewers]] makes a loud squeak that echoes through the tunnels and should trigger an encounter check.

| d6 | Encounter |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | A patrol of [[Fintooth's Minions]]. |
| 2 | Slime under the water. See [[Creatures of the Sewers]]. |
| 3 | A carrion crawler at a dead end. |
| 4 | A giant rat leaping from a hole in the wall. |
| 5 | A giant spider dropping from a ceiling shaft. |
| 6 | Rats, snakes, centipedes and spiders. |
