## Personality

- Morbidly curious about people's fascination with dragons that don't exist.
- Deflects every personal question.
- **Vehemently ridicules** any suggestion that dragons actually exist.
- Generous with information and coin when it buys him a good story.

## Motivation

**What he knows:**
- The [[Order of the Orchid]] seeks out and destroys statues of dragons.
- The Order is often racing some other, unknown group to reach them.
- He once tracked a troupe of knights into a dense forest, where they found and destroyed an out-of-place, enormous boulder covered in vines and moss. After they left, he spent days searching the rubble and realized it had been an ancient, weather-worn statue of a dragon.

**What he believes:**
- The Order is a benevolent organization acting to save the world.
- So the other organization must be nefarious.

**What he suspects:**
- The dragon statues hold or enable some great power.
- The Order wants to keep that power out of the wrong hands.

**What he wants to discover:**
- Where do these statues come from?
- Why does the Order want to destroy them?
- Who is the Order racing to find them, and what does that group want with them?
- He also covets the [[Dragon Claw Daggers]] for his personal prestige, and will pay handsomely.

## Secrets

> [!secret]- GM Only
> - His name is **Simeon Chesterguile**.
> - He is good at tracking (perhaps a ranger), at stealth and disguise (perhaps a rogue), and maybe even at illusion.
> - He always carries a vial of [[Dragon Blood]], hidden deep in the folds of his clothing. For a man who mocks belief in dragons, that's quite a thing to own.
> - **His intended role:** the arbiter of balance between the [[Order of the Orchid]] and the [[Calla Cabal]]. He encourages the party not to take sides and helps them fill in the parts of the story they're missing. He should pop up after every major dragon-related encounter ([[We Meet Again]]).

## Relationships

- [[Order of the Orchid]]: the object of his decades-long obsession.
- [[Calla Cabal]]: he suspects it exists, but not what it is.
- [[The Slattern Tavern]]: where he hosts his dinners.
