## Secrets

> [!secret]- GM Only
> - His name is **Eldegar** (no last name).
> - When [[The Stoning]] struck, **one dragon** happened to be on another plane of existence and was spared. That was him.
> - On discovering what had happened, he **polymorphed into a human** and has stayed that way for a thousand years.
> - In human form he has the strength of a barbarian and the spell power of a wizard.
> - The hooked dagger is made of a dragon's claw, though not his own.
>
> **Possible directions (undecided):**
> - He has lost his memories from before the Stoning and doesn't know he's a dragon.
> - In dragon form he was a friend or respected associate of [[The Savior]]. Out of forbidden love or simple respect, he was warned about the Stoning and advised to be "off world" when it happened.
>
