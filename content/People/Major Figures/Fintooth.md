*A grey-skinned shark-man who runs a gang of minions from a driftwood throne in the sewers, and talks like the Godfather.*

## Appearance

- **See:** a grey-skinned shark-man with a human body and a shark's head, seated on a throne of driftwood amid medieval refuse.
- **Hear:** he speaks like The Godfather.
- **Smell:**

## Personality

- A recluse.
- A negotiator: when his life is on the line, he makes an offer you can't refuse.

## Motivation

- Survive. He fled the sea after making some enemies, but he still needs to live in water. He swam up [[The River]] and found the town's sewer outlet. (Don't get hung up on salt versus fresh water.)
- Build a small army of [[Fintooth's Minions]] and send them on small raids for barrels of fish.

## Secrets

> [!secret]- GM Only
> - If cornered, he offers a **map to the untold treasures of [[Dragon Reef]]** in exchange for his life. He has been there, in his open-sea days.
> - He carries [[Fintooth's Trident]], which helps him control his minions.
> - **His hoard** sits in a compartment beneath the seat of his driftwood throne: jewelry, gems and coins paid as tribute by the minions, plus the treasure map and the [[Sapphire of the Sea]].
> - [[Turd the Troll]] is an unwitting ally, and some minions worship the troll.

## Relationships

- [[Fintooth's Minions]]: his army.
- [[Turd the Troll]]: unwitting ally.
- [[Gill Herring]]: his raids are the fishermen's guild's problem.
