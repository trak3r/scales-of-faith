*A self-titled halfling expert on dragons, which, he insists, are invisible and silent.*

## Appearance

- **See:** a halfling dressed fancy, like a college professor.
- **Hear:**
- **Smell:**

## Personality

- A believer, and a salesman. He gave himself the title "Dragonologist."

## Motivation

- Convince others that he's an expert on dragons.
- Convince others, and himself, that dragons exist.
- His theory: **dragons are invisible and silent**. A breeze is their wings as they fly nearby. Shaking ground is one landing or walking past.

## Secrets

> [!secret]- GM Only
> His "invisible dragon" is a [[Bulette]].

## Relationships

- Advertises around town with **posters**.
- Offers **consultations** on dragon matters for a fee, plus a **"dragon experience" field trip**.
