*A goblin jeweler in a tailored suit who talks like a Joe Pesci gangster.*

## Appearance

- **See:** a goblin in a finely tailored suit, with non-corrective spectacles worn purely for show.
- **Hear:** a Joe Pesci gangster.
- **Smell:** heavy cologne.

## Personality

- Loves pretty gems and hates to sell them. He accepts that selling the smaller, cheaper ones pays for bigger, shinier ones.

## Motivation

- Complete his jewel dice set (see [[Jerem's Jewelry]]).

## Secrets

> [!secret]- GM Only
> - His loupe **detects magic**, and he'll try to low-ball powerful items.
> - He's probably colluding with [[Nick]].
