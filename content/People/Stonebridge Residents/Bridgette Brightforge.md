*A dwarf blacksmith hiding a masterwork talent, and a past, in a nondescript town.*

## Appearance

- **See:** a female dwarf at a sweltering forge.
- **Hear:** the clang of hammer on anvil. *"Olen's mercs buy all my good stuff. If you want something now, you'll have to dig through the barrels."*
- **Smell:** burning wood, coal and molten iron.

## Personality

- Polite, and politely evasive about her past.

## Motivation

- Stay hidden.

## Secrets

> [!secret]- GM Only
> - She can craft **masterwork items** with inherent, non-magical +1 bonuses to armor, attack or damage, but she deliberately makes mundane-quality goods to protect her identity.
> - She chose this humble shop in this nondescript town to **hide from her past**.
> - She will do or pay anything to protect her true identity, including abandoning the shop in the middle of the night and fleeing again.
> - When a posse of heavily armed dwarves comes looking for her, the party must choose a side.

## Relationships

- [[Brightforge Smithy]]: her shop.
- [[Olen]]: his mercenaries buy all her good stock.
- [[Shamrock Loams]]: probably knows her secret.
