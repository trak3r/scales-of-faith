*The best hireling in town: chipper, tireless, and always comes home unscathed.*

## Appearance

- **See:** a perky, rambunctious young lad, dirty, with freckles, unkempt hair and tattered clothes.
- **Hear:** sings during travel.
- **Smell:**

## Personality

- Energetic and chipper, always positive, never complains.

## Motivation

- He longs to be a proper **knight's squire**.

## Services

- Sets up and tears down camp, tends the horses, cooks meals and sings on the road.
- **No combat skills.** He runs and hides from danger.
- Moderate hunting and snaring skills, with a low chance of catching small game.
- **Will not enter dungeons or caves.** He's happy to stay with the horses.
- **Fee:** 2 silver pieces per day. Tips are expected for exceptional service.

## Secrets

> [!secret]- GM Only
> - He has made a name for himself by **always returning from an adventure unscathed**.
> - Once he returned alone: *"after three days and three nights, the dungeon-delving adventurers failed to resurface, and he sadly packed up and headed home."* That may be the same party that comes back in [[The Party Returns]].
