*An aged dwarven former monk, master brewer and financial genius, who can drink anyone under the table.*

## Appearance

- **See:** an aged dwarf, mug of ale in hand, surrounded by barrels.
- **Hear:** a loud, booming voice.
- **Smell:** the brewery. You can smell it a mile away.

## Personality

- Can drink anybody under the table.
