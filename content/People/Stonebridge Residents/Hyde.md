## Personality

- Protective of the townsfolk, even from himself.

## Motivation

- Keep his secret, and keep everyone safe from his violent alter ego.
- Make a dire bear cloak for a local noble ([[Hunt for the Dire Bear]]). He'll make about 20 gp on the finished cloak, so what he pays the party depends on how much profit he's willing to give up and the quality of the pelt, probably around 6 gp.

## Secrets

> [!secret]- GM Only
> - Hyde is a **lycanthrope (were-rat)**.
> - Every full moon he buys a slab of meat from [[Ground and Pound]] and locks himself in a cell in [[The Sewers]]. If he's discovered there, he's surrounded by small sewer rats, like a pied piper. See [[Full Moon]].
> - He owns a suit of leather armor made from **displacer beast hide**, which gives him the same benefits in combat. He keeps it in a locked, trapped chest behind his bed.
> - The [[Courier Druids]] know about his lycanthropy.
