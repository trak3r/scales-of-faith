*A lone Knight of the Order on a time-sensitive holy quest, short of companions.*

## Appearance

- **See:** black-enameled plate armor and a mighty two-handed maul.
- **Hear:** says little. He deflects questions about his quest.
- **Smell:**

## Personality

- Disciplined and private.
- Always awake before the rest of the party.
- Reads an ancient scroll and several pages of his holy book every night, then packs them in the knapsack he uses as a pillow.

## Motivation

- Reach the cavern before his rivals do.

## Secrets

> [!secret]- GM Only
> - The scroll tells of the dragon that lived in [[The Dragon Cavern]].
> - His mission is to smash its [[Stone Dragon|statue]] with a *Holy Word of Power*.
> - If questioned about the trap on the way home, he admits it was set for him and that he knows by whom, but gives no details (it was the [[Calla Cabal]]).
