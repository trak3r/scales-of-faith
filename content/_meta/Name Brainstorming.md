The project started as **Imagine Dragons**, but that name is taken by a famous band. The wiki is now titled **Scales of Faith**. The original brainstorm is kept here for reference.

## Constraint

Something that *doesn't* evoke the band.

## Word Banks

- **Dragon:** wyrm, wyvern, drake, hydra
- **Imagine:** cryptid, myth, ambiguous, fable, dream, dubious, mirage, faith
- conspire, deceit, facade, fraud, deception, bluff, faux, hoax
- feint, fate
- fade, forget, doubt
- **Spoilers?** disappear, vanish, return, revenge

## Portmanteaus

- **Wyrmhaze** (a synonym for confusion; good for search)
- Dragonmere (sounds too familiar)
- Dragonfaith
- Wyrmyth

## Miscellany

- **Scales of Faith** (also suggested as a faction name)
  - scales like a dragon's skin
  - scales like a balance of good and bad
  - faith as belief in something unprovable
- Dragon's Doubt (liked this one)
- Dragon's Bluff (could be a location name)
- Dragon's Deceit
- Dragon's Feint

## Unused Candidates for Later

Dragon's Bluff, Wyrmhaze and Dragon's Doubt are still free for a place, a faction, a sequel or a module title.
