This wiki is written for **game masters**. Everything is public, including spoilers, but secrets are tucked inside collapsed boxes, so you can skim a page with your players nearby.

## Callouts

> [!secret]- GM Only
> Collapsed boxes like this hold the hidden truth about a person, place or event. Click to expand.

> [!prep]
> Prep boxes list the **maps** and **tokens** you'll want ready before running a scene.

> [!todo]
> To-do boxes mark gaps the author hasn't filled yet. Treat them as invitations to improvise.

> [!map] Map placeholder: Example
> A map is planned for this spot but hasn't been made yet.

## Page Status

Every page is tagged with a status:

- **complete**: ready to run as written.
- **partial**: usable, with some gaps marked as to-dos.
- **stub**: an idea with little detail. Flesh it out before play.

## How Pages Are Organized

- **Places** describe *where*: history, description, inhabitants. **Adventures** describe *what happens there*. A site like [[Dragon Reef]] has a place page, and the scenario that unfolds there, [[Raising the Reef]], has its own adventure page.
- **People** get their own pages. Businesses link to their proprietors, and proprietors link back to their shops.
- One-scene characters, such as the bandits in [[In Media Res]], live inside their adventure. Everyone is listed in the [[Dramatis Personae]].
- Small shop side quests are collected in [[Rumors and Hooks]].

## System Neutrality

No stat blocks. Creature pages describe behavior and tactics and suggest a stock monster from whatever bestiary you use ("use any giant spider"). Checks are written plainly ("a hard perception check") so you can translate them into your system.

## Names

Some adventure titles, such as *Raising the Reef* or *Journey to U'gachaka*, are working titles.
