> [!secret]- GM Only: the whole story
> **The truth.** Long ago, dragons were real. During the [[The War of the Wyrm|War of the Wyrm]], men and dragons fought for centuries. It ended when a nameless wizard, [[The Savior]], cast a single spell that turned every living dragon to stone in an instant. The spell left him a vegetable, and he was hidden away and forgotten. See [[The Stoning]].
>
> **The aftermath.** A holy book was written about the war and The Savior, and zealots built a church around it: the [[Order of the Orchid]]. With no living dragons left to fight, the Order made it its mission to find and smash every [[The Stone Dragons|stone dragon]]. Centuries later nobody believes in dragons, so the Order keeps that mission secret ([[The Purge]]).
>
> **The opposition.** A defector from the Order came to believe dragons were vital to the balance of nature. The [[Calla Cabal]] they founded races the Order to each statue, revives the dragon, and sends it to the [[The Plane of Dreams|Plane of Dreams]] until the world is safe for its return.
>
> **The wildcards.**
> - [[The Sage]] is a wealthy investigator who has spied on the Order for decades. He doesn't believe in dragons, but he badly wants to know why anyone smashes statues.
> - [[The Survivor]] is the one dragon who was on another plane when the spell struck. He has lived in human form ever since.
>
> The timeline is still open: the notes say both "millennia ago" and "a thousand years."
>
> **The endgame.** In the Plane of Dreams, the revived dragons argue over the fate of mankind. Some want vengeance for the genocide. Others remember that mankind (the Cabal) saved them. The argument becomes a civil war that spills into the real world. See [[Fate of the Dragons]].

## Structure

The campaign is a **sandbox around a spine**:

- **The spine** is a series of dragon statues ([[The Stone Dragons]]). At each one the party meets the Order, the Cabal or both, and eventually has to choose a side ([[Faction Loyalty]]).
- **The sandbox** is the home base of [[Stonebridge]], a remote river town full of shops, eccentric NPCs and side quests that keep the party busy between expeditions.

See [[Campaign Arc]] for the intended order of adventures.

## Tone

The world is played straight. The twist does the heavy lifting, and humor comes from the people: a gangster goblin jeweler, a self-titled dragonologist, a wrestling troupe that plays heroes and villains.
